# Skeptic — D1 open decisions
Lens: what's asserted but unproven; what's assumed someone else covered.

- [BLOCKER] Fan Layer 2 tax documents are labelled 1099-MISC. Mechanism 04 Decision 1 recommends a C-corp-taxed issuer issuing **1099-DIV / 1099-INT**, and regulatory.html §8 says "generally 1099-DIV". The label was invented and contradicts both. Ref: src/pages/settings/TaxDocuments.tsx:22-23. Behind `layer2`, but it is the exact "assumed, not decided" pattern Mechanism 04 warns kills platforms.
- [CONCERN] Even 1099-DIV is not settled: Mechanism 04 lists "income characterization (royalty vs interest vs dividend vs return-of-capital)" as an open counsel item. The UI should show the form as "1099 (type confirmed by tax counsel)" until characterization is decided — or show 1099-DIV with that caveat.
- [CONCERN] Holder votes assume an SPV structure that counsel hasn't blessed (Mechanism 03 §12(g) relief "DISPUTED pending counsel") and that is itself gated behind the reward-vs-profit-share A/B. Building UI for it now spends design attention on the least-validated corner of the product. Ref: src/pages/invest/HolderVotes.tsx.
- [CONCERN] Card processing "passed through to artists" (fees.html) is asserted for *investment transfers* too. On Reg CF purchases, fees paid by issuers to the intermediary/processor must be disclosed in the Form C; nobody has checked whether passing processing to the artist on investment flows changes Form C disclosure. Fine for Layer 1; flag for Layer 2.
- [CONCERN] Engagement badges ("Five campaigns backed") are a gamified nudge toward spending. Not securities, but PRD 03 market-integrity cares about wash-funding: badges must not count self-backing by artists or linked accounts.
- Strongest reason not to proceed: every one of these decisions sits on top of the reward-vs-profit-share A/B test that hasn't run. Layer 2 and post-Beta UI (tax forms, holder votes, transfers) are being specified in detail for a path the council itself gated — the prototype risks making the gated path *feel* decided.
