# Critic — D1 open decisions
Lens: is the experience actually good and honest, not merely complete?

- [BLOCKER] The For Artists page tells artists Established/Pro "criteria published before this tier opens" while the creator dashboard shows a "Path to Established" with different, made-up numbers. An artist comparing the two sees a platform that doesn't know its own rules — the opposite of Brand "Trusted". Both must show the PRD 01 §6.3 criteria. Ref: ForArtists.tsx:32-44, creator/Dashboard.tsx:279-290.
- [CONCERN] Fan engagement badges are the right call over dollar tiers (Brand §7: honest, not hype; PRD 02: never rank by money), but the All-Access card currently reads like a status object. Keep it as membership identity (member since, artists backed), never a spend-based level. Ref: src/pages/fan/Profile.tsx.
- [CONCERN] Fan checkout copy "no fees added" (because artists absorb processing) is good UX, but the artist side must show it clearly at campaign setup: "Card processing (~2.9% + $0.30) comes out of what you raise." Otherwise artists get surprised at payout. Ref: campaign wizard Basics/Preview, creator Payouts.
- [NIT] Milestone 40/35/25 demo reads fine to a user, but differs from the wizard's 50/50 default — someone testing both will notice.
- [NIT] Holder votes UI is well-crafted but its quality makes it look launch-ready; if kept, the postBeta gate copy should be the only thing users can reach.
