# FanZuP Services Marketplace — Provider Terms

> **DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW — NOT LEGAL ADVICE — NOT FOR PUBLICATION.** First draft, 2026-11-27. Written to match Phase MKT-1 of the marketplace (CR-005, `specs/marketplace/01-requirements.md`, ADR-010): a directory, listings, service requests and responses, with **no payments through FanZuP and no fees**. Sections 12 and 13 describe what changes if paid bookings or fees are added later; those must be rewritten after counsel answers MKT-Q1…Q8.

**Version:** [2026-xx-xx] · **Effective:** [date] · **Operator:** FanZuP LLC, a Virginia limited liability company ("FanZuP", "we", "us")

These Provider Terms apply when you create a provider profile or listing on the FanZuP services marketplace (the "Marketplace") to offer services to artists. They add to the FanZuP [Terms of Service](01-terms-of-service.md), [Acceptable Use and Community Guidelines](07-acceptable-use-policy.md) and [Privacy Notice](02-privacy-notice.md), which also apply to you. If these Provider Terms conflict with the Terms of Service on a Marketplace matter, these Provider Terms control.

## Summary (plain language, not a substitute for the terms below)
- FanZuP lists vetted providers so artists can find you and send you requests. That's all FanZuP does today.
- **FanZuP does not take, hold or send any money for your services, and charges you nothing during the beta.** You and the artist agree the price, scope and payment yourselves, outside FanZuP.
- FanZuP isn't a party to your deal with an artist and doesn't guarantee either side.
- You're an independent business. You're responsible for your own work, taxes, licences and insurance.
- We review your profile before it's listed and can stop listing you if you break these terms.

## 1. Who can be a provider

1.1 You must be at least 18, hold a verified FanZuP account, and be able to form a binding contract. If you sign up for a business, you confirm you're authorized to bind it, and "you" includes that business.

1.2 One provider profile per account during the beta. You may also have an artist profile on the same account, but you can't send requests to yourself.

1.3 You must not be a person or entity subject to U.S. sanctions, or located in a comprehensively sanctioned country or region. *(Open question MKT-Q8: sanctions and identity screening are required before any payout; in MKT-1 FanZuP makes no payouts. Attorney: confirm whether a self-attestation is enough while no money moves.)*

## 2. Your profile and listings

2.1 **What you provide.** Your profile includes a display name, headline, bio, city, whether you work remotely, up to five service categories, up to five portfolio links (https only), and a contact email. Each listing includes a category, title, description, an optional "starting at" price with a unit (per project, song, hour or day), and an optional typical turnaround.

2.2 **Accuracy.** Everything you submit must be true, current and yours to share. You'll describe your experience, credits and portfolio honestly and won't claim credits, affiliations, awards or results you don't have. Portfolio links must point to work you made or are authorized to show, with credit to collaborators where they'd expect it.

2.3 **"Starting at" prices are guides, not offers.** A listed price is a starting point for a conversation with the artist. It is not a binding offer, a quote, a fee, a deposit or a charge, and FanZuP doesn't collect it. The price you and an artist agree is set between you.

2.4 **Prohibited services.** You may not list or provide: paid playlist placement, paid streaming or chart manipulation, bought followers, plays, views or reviews; services that infringe anyone's rights; anything illegal; or anything in the Acceptable Use and Community Guidelines' prohibited list. *(Paid placement is excluded because it breaks most streaming services' terms, FR-MKT-002.)*

2.5 **Licence to FanZuP.** You grant FanZuP a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to host, display, reproduce, format and promote your profile, listings, name and portfolio links on FanZuP's web and mobile apps and in FanZuP's marketing of the Marketplace, for as long as your profile is listed and a reasonable time after to remove it from materials already distributed. You keep ownership of your content. *(Attorney: confirm scope of the marketing use and the post-removal tail.)*

## 3. Review and listing

3.1 **Staff review.** Profiles are reviewed by FanZuP staff before they're shown to anyone. A profile moves from draft to in review when you submit it, and then to listed or changes requested. Editing the public fields of a listed profile sends it back to review.

3.2 **What review means.** Review checks that a profile looks genuine and fits these terms. **It is not an endorsement, certification, background check, licence check or guarantee of your skills, availability or work,** and FanZuP won't describe it to artists as one. *(Attorney: confirm the wording artists see, e.g. "reviewed by FanZuP" rather than "verified" or "vetted", to avoid implying a guarantee.)*

3.3 **Order.** Listings are shown most recently listed first. No listing is ranked by payment, and FanZuP doesn't sell placement during the beta.

3.4 **Our discretion.** We may decline, ask for changes to, hide or suspend any profile or listing, with a note, if we reasonably believe it breaks these terms, is inaccurate, creates risk for artists or FanZuP, or for any other reason permitted by law. When a profile is suspended it is hidden immediately and any open requests to it are closed as declined with the note "This provider is no longer listed."

## 4. Service requests

4.1 An artist with a verified account and an artist profile may send you a request against one of your listings, with a brief, an optional budget, an optional needed-by date and, optionally, one of their FanZuP campaigns. A budget is for information only.

4.2 You can accept or decline a request, with an optional note. **Accepting a request is not a contract to do the work**; it means you're willing to talk terms. Any contract to provide services is made directly between you and the artist.

4.3 When you accept, FanZuP shows the artist your contact email, and shows you the artist's account email only if the artist chose to share it. When you decline, nothing is shared.

4.4 FanZuP limits how many requests an artist can send (currently 10 per day, and one open request per artist per listing) to reduce spam. FanZuP doesn't promise you any number of requests.

## 5. Your relationship with artists

5.1 **Your contract, not ours.** You and the artist are solely responsible for agreeing the scope, deliverables, schedule, price, payment method, revisions, cancellation, credit and ownership or licensing of the work. **We strongly encourage a written agreement for every job.** FanZuP is not a party to it and has no obligation under it.

5.2 **Ownership of deliverables.** Who owns masters, stems, mixes, compositions, lyrics, footage, photographs and artwork you create is for you and the artist to agree in writing. FanZuP takes no ownership of, and no licence to, deliverables you make for an artist. *(Open question MKT-Q6. Attorney: decide whether FanZuP should require the parties to address ownership, offer a template work-for-hire/licence addendum, or state a default rule. Consider songwriter and producer splits, session musician releases, union (AFM/SAG-AFTRA) issues and sync/likeness rights for video and photo.)*

5.3 **Payment happens off FanZuP.** FanZuP does not process, hold, guarantee or refund any payment between you and an artist. Methods you agree with the artist (invoice, bank transfer, payment app, cash) are at your and the artist's risk. FanZuP offers no payment protection, dispute resolution or chargeback handling for Marketplace work during the beta.

5.4 **Campaign funds.** If an artist links a request to a FanZuP campaign, that link is for context only. FanZuP never pays you from a campaign, and you must not ask an artist to direct backers' money to you or describe your services to backers as something FanZuP guarantees. *(Open question MKT-Q3.)*

5.5 **Professional conduct.** You'll respond to requests in good faith, keep commitments you make to artists, deliver the work you agree to, and treat artists' unreleased music, lyrics, footage, personal information and plans as confidential unless they agree otherwise.

## 6. Independent business

6.1 You're an independent business, not an employee, agent, partner or joint venturer of FanZuP. FanZuP doesn't direct or control how, when or where you work, set your prices, or require you to accept any request.

6.2 You're responsible for your own tools, staff, subcontractors, expenses, insurance and any business licences, permits or union obligations that apply to your services.

## 7. Taxes

7.1 You're responsible for all taxes on what you earn from artists, including income and self-employment tax, and for collecting and remitting any sales, use or similar tax that applies to your services.

7.2 Because FanZuP doesn't process payments for your services in the beta, **FanZuP does not issue Forms 1099 to providers for Marketplace work.** *(Open questions MKT-Q4 and MKT-Q5 apply when payments come through FanZuP.)*

## 8. Personal information

8.1 FanZuP's [Privacy Notice](02-privacy-notice.md) explains how FanZuP handles your information, including what artists see.

8.2 You may use an artist's personal information that you receive through the Marketplace (name, brief, linked campaign title, and email if shared) **only to respond to that request and to provide and bill for the services you agree**. You may not add artists to marketing lists, sell or share their information, or contact them for unrelated purposes without their consent. You'll keep it secure and delete it when you no longer need it for those purposes or legal recordkeeping. *(Attorney: confirm whether this makes the provider an independent controller and whether any state-law processor/contractor terms are needed.)*

## 9. Your promises
You represent and warrant that: (a) the information you give FanZuP is accurate; (b) you have the rights to everything you post and to the portfolio work you show; (c) you'll comply with all laws that apply to your services, including consumer protection, advertising, intellectual property, privacy, tax and licensing laws; and (d) you hold any licence, certification or insurance your services legally require.

10.2 FanZuP's limitation of liability in the Terms of Service applies to the Marketplace. *(Attorney: because no money flows through FanZuP, consider a low fixed cap, e.g. USD [100], for Marketplace claims.)*

10.3 **Release.** To the extent the law allows, you release FanZuP from claims arising out of any dispute between you and an artist, including about work, payment, ownership or conduct. *(Attorney: California Civil Code §1542 waiver language if appropriate.)*

## 11. Indemnity
You'll defend, indemnify and hold harmless FanZuP and its members, managers, employees and agents from third-party claims, losses and reasonable legal fees arising from your services, your content, your agreements with artists, your breach of these terms, or your violation of law or anyone's rights. *(Attorney: confirm mutuality and scope appropriate for small independent providers.)*

## 12. Fees

12.1 **The Marketplace is free for providers and artists during the beta.** FanZuP charges no listing fee, subscription, commission or referral fee.

12.2 If FanZuP introduces fees, it will give you at least [30] days' notice and the fee terms before they apply, and you can remove your profile before then. No fee will be charged for requests accepted before the fee takes effect. *(Fee model is TBD: Wayne's decision D-MKT-2 sets it after the beta.)*

## 13. If paid bookings are added later
FanZuP may later let artists pay providers through FanZuP (quotes, bookings, delivery confirmation and payouts). That will only happen under separate payment terms you'll be asked to accept, after identity, tax and sanctions checks. Until you accept those terms, these Provider Terms don't authorize FanZuP to collect, hold or pay any money on your behalf. *(Open questions MKT-Q1, Q2, Q4, Q8. Per ADR-010, any such flow will run through a licensed processor or custodian; counsel picks the legal flow of funds.)*

## 14. Reviews and ratings
There are no reviews or ratings in the beta. If they're added, FanZuP will only collect them after a completed job and will follow the FTC rule on consumer reviews and testimonials (16 CFR Part 465). You must never write, buy, solicit in exchange for anything of value, or suppress reviews. *(Open question MKT-Q7.)*

## 15. Ending your participation

15.1 You may unlist or delete your provider profile at any time. Requests you've already accepted remain your and the artist's responsibility.

15.2 FanZuP may suspend or remove your profile at any time under section 3.4 or the Terms of Service. FanZuP may also end the Marketplace or change it; it will give reasonable notice where practical.

15.3 Sections 5, 6, 7, 8.2, 9, 10, 11, 15 and 17 survive.

## 16. Changes to these terms
We may update these Provider Terms. For material changes we'll give at least [30] days' notice by email or in the app, and may require you to accept the new version before you can keep your profile listed. Acceptance is recorded with the version and time.

## 17. Disputes, governing law, notices
Sections 15 (Disputes) and 16 (General, including governing law) of the Terms of Service apply. Notices to FanZuP: [legal@fanzup.com], FanZuP LLC, [physical address, Virginia]. *(Open question P-2.)*
