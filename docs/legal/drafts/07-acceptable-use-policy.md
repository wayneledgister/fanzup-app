# FanZuP Acceptable Use and Community Guidelines

> **DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW — NOT LEGAL ADVICE — NOT FOR PUBLICATION.** First draft, 2026-11-27. Incorporated into the Terms of Service; used by staff when reviewing campaigns (FR-CMP-002), suspending them (FR-CMP-007) and vetting marketplace providers (FR-MKT-008). Prohibited-campaign categories should be checked against the chosen payment processor's restricted-business list.

**Version:** [2026-xx-xx] · **Effective:** [date]

FanZuP exists so fans can trust artists with real money. These rules keep it that way. They apply to everyone: fans, artists and service providers.

## 1. Be honest
- Don't misrepresent who you are, your credits, your project, your progress or how funds will be used.
- Campaigns must be for a real music project you intend and are able to complete: a show, tour, album, music video or documentary.
- Don't present backing as an investment, or promise returns, profits, revenue shares, royalties or ownership.
- Don't post fake updates, fake milestone evidence, or fake reviews or testimonials.

## 2. Don't game the platform
- Don't back your own campaign, or use friends' or linked accounts, to inflate totals, badges or rankings.
- Don't test cards, use stolen payment methods, or file disputes you know are false.
- Don't buy or sell accounts, or create accounts to get around a suspension or limit.
- Don't scrape, spider, overload, reverse-engineer or probe the Services' security, or access accounts or data that aren't yours. Report security issues to [security@fanzup.com].

## 3. Respect people
- No harassment, threats, hate speech, doxxing or incitement to violence.
- No sexual content involving minors, ever. No sexually explicit content in campaigns, perks or profiles.
- Use other people's personal information only for the purpose they gave it to you. Artists may use backer information only to deliver perks for that campaign; providers and artists may use each other's contact details only to arrange the requested work.
- Don't send spam or unsolicited marketing through FanZuP.

## 4. Respect rights
- Only post music, lyrics, images, video, names, logos and likenesses you own or have permission to use. Covers need the right licences.
- See the [Copyright and DMCA Policy](09-copyright-dmca-policy.md) to report infringement.

## 5. Prohibited campaigns and perks
Campaigns and perks may not involve:
- securities, investment opportunities, lotteries, raffles, sweepstakes or gambling *(attorney: confirm whether prize-style perks need sweepstakes review)*;
- weapons, drugs, drug paraphernalia, tobacco or vaping products, or alcohol *(attorney: alcohol at events and as perks)*;
- anything illegal where the artist or backers are, or that requires a licence the artist doesn't hold;
- fundraising for charities, political campaigns or personal debts not tied to a music project;
- items or services in [the payment processor's prohibited and restricted businesses list];
- perks the artist can't legally deliver (for example, recordings they don't own).

## 6. Services marketplace rules
- Providers must describe their services, credits and portfolio honestly and follow the [Provider Terms](05-provider-terms.md).
- **No paid playlist placement, paid streaming or chart manipulation, or bought followers, plays, views or reviews.**
- Don't list services you aren't qualified or permitted to provide.
- Don't use requests to spam providers; FanZuP limits requests to [10] per user per day.
- Don't ask artists to send backers' money to you, or present your services as backed by FanZuP.

## 7. Enforcement
We may remove content, ask for changes, limit features, suspend or cancel campaigns, hide provider profiles, hold funds as the Artist Campaign Agreement allows, or close accounts. We'll usually tell you why, unless the law or safety prevents it. Serious or repeated violations may be reported to law enforcement or our payment partners. If you think we got it wrong, write to [support@fanzup.com] within 30 days.

## 8. Report a problem
Use "Report" on any campaign, profile or listing, or email [trust@fanzup.com].
