FanZuP respects artists' and creators' rights and expects users to do the same. We respond to notices of alleged copyright infringement under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. §512).

## 1. Reporting infringement
If you believe content on FanZuP infringes your copyright, send our designated agent a written notice that includes:
1. your physical or electronic signature (or that of the person authorized to act for the owner);
2. identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed (or a representative list if there are several);
3. identification of the material you claim is infringing and enough information to locate it (for example the URL of the campaign, profile or listing);
4. your contact information (address, phone number and email);
5. a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use isn't authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law; and
6. a statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.

**Designated agent:** [Name], FanZuP LLC, [physical address], [dmca@fanzup.com], [phone]. *(Must match the Copyright Office registration.)*

## 2. What we do
We'll remove or disable access to the material promptly, notify the user who posted it, and give them a copy of the notice. If a whole campaign is affected, we may pause it while the issue is resolved; backers' rights under the Refund and Cancellation Policy aren't affected.

## 3. Counter-notice
If your content was removed and you believe it was a mistake or misidentification, you can send a counter-notice to our designated agent with:
1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. identification of the material removed and where it appeared;
3. a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief it was removed by mistake or misidentification;
4. your name, address and phone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address (or, if outside the U.S., any district where FanZuP may be found) and will accept service from the person who sent the original notice.

We'll forward it to the complainant and restore the material in 10 to 14 business days unless they tell us they've filed a court action.

## 4. Repeat infringers
We'll close the accounts of users who are repeat infringers in appropriate circumstances. *(Attorney: define the strike threshold, e.g. three valid notices in 12 months.)*

## 5. Misrepresentations
Anyone who knowingly makes a material misrepresentation in a notice or counter-notice may be liable for damages under 17 U.S.C. §512(f).

## 6. Other intellectual property
For trademark, right-of-publicity or other complaints, email [legal@fanzup.com] with the same detail.

## 7. Marketplace deliverables
FanZuP doesn't host deliverables exchanged between artists and providers. Disputes over who owns work made by a provider are between the artist and the provider (see the Provider Terms).
