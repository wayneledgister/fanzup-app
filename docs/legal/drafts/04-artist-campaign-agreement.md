# FanZuP Artist Campaign Agreement

> **DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW — NOT LEGAL ADVICE — NOT FOR PUBLICATION.** First draft, 2026-11-27. Accepted by artists before submitting a campaign (FR-PRV-001). **Platform fees are TBD** (`docs/brand/fees.html`); only card processing pass-through is stated. Custody, holdback and 1099 clauses depend on Brief Q2, Q5, Q7 and E1 card C.

**Version:** [2026-xx-xx] · **Effective:** [date]

This agreement is between you (the artist, or the business you represent) and FanZuP LLC ("FanZuP"). It applies to every campaign you run and adds to the [Terms of Service](01-terms-of-service.md).

## Summary
- You run a time-limited, target-or-refund campaign. If you miss your goal, every backer gets a full refund and you receive nothing.
- If you hit your goal, funds are released to you in stages: part on funding, the rest when FanZuP verifies the milestones you set.
- You're responsible for delivering every perk you promise, by the date you promise, and for being honest with fans.
- You may use backers' details only to deliver that campaign's perks and to talk about that campaign.

## 1. Eligibility

1.1 You must be 18 or older (confirmed by identity verification), have a verified FanZuP account and an artist profile, and complete identity, tax and payout onboarding before any funds are released. You can't receive funds if you, or anyone controlling your business, is subject to U.S. sanctions.

1.2 If you act for a band, group or business, you confirm you're authorized to bind all of them and you're responsible for how funds are shared among them. *(Attorney: consider requiring a designated payee and band-member acknowledgment.)*

## 2. Your campaign

2.1 **Contents.** Your campaign states its type (show, tour, album, music video or documentary), funding goal, deadline (between [7] and [60] days from going live), story, use of funds, risks, perks with quantities and expected delivery dates, and 2–3 tranches with the share of funds released at each: the first on funding and 1–2 more, each released after FanZuP verifies a milestone (by default 50% on funding and 50% after one verified milestone).

2.2 **Truthfulness.** Everything on your campaign must be accurate and not misleading, and must be **your own words and plans**. Describe risks honestly. Don't promise anything you can't deliver, and don't describe a backing as an investment, a return, ownership, royalties or a revenue share. *(FanZuP's copy rules ban investment language in Layer 1.)*

2.3 **Review.** FanZuP reviews every campaign before it goes live (usually within 2 business days) and may ask for changes. Approval isn't an endorsement or a guarantee. After approval you choose when to go live; the deadline is set from that moment.

2.4 **Stretch goals** never change the funding goal used for target-or-refund.

2.5 **Changes while live.** You may not lower perks' value, push delivery dates or change milestones once backers have relied on them without telling backers and, where this agreement says so, offering refunds.

## 3. Money

3.1 **FanZuP doesn't hold funds.** Backers' payments are processed and held by [processor/custodian]. You authorize FanZuP to instruct them to hold, refund and release funds under this agreement. *(Brief Q2; describe the final flow of funds.)*

3.2 **Target-or-refund.** If your campaign doesn't reach its goal by the deadline, every backer is refunded in full and you receive nothing.

3.3 **Releases.** If your campaign is funded, the first tranche is released after funding, and each later tranche after FanZuP verifies the milestone evidence you submit. Verified tranches are paid within [1 business day] unless a hold applies. FanZuP may hold a release if: a reconciliation break is open; your campaign is suspended; disputes on your campaign exceed [threshold]; your tax information is missing; or identity or sanctions checks fail or need to be repeated. *(FR-PAY-005/007, FR-DSP-004, FR-TAX-001/003.)*

3.4 **Fees.** FanZuP's platform fee is **[TBD]**, charged only on successfully funded campaigns and shown before you submit. Card processing costs ([2.9% + $0.30] per transaction at current rates) are passed through to you at cost. All deductions appear on your statement as separate lines. *(Attorney: confirm fee and pass-through structure; fees must be decided before this agreement is final.)*

3.5 **Holdback.** FanZuP may hold back [x]% of your first release for [y] days to cover refunds and chargebacks on your campaign. *(E1 card C / Brief Q5: size and window to set with counsel.)*

3.6 **Refunds and chargebacks after funding.** If a backer is refunded after funding (for example a late perk or a problem report) or wins a chargeback, the amount comes first from funds not yet released to you, then from the holdback, and you agree to repay any remaining amount [and the dispute fee] to FanZuP. FanZuP may set it off against future releases. *(E1 card C.)*

3.7 **Payout account changes** take effect only after a security delay, and FanZuP may pause releases while a change is verified.

3.8 **Use of funds.** Use released funds for the project as described. *(MKT-Q3: whether funds still held for backers may ever pay a third-party provider directly; until counsel answers, they may not.)*

## 4. Taxes

4.1 You're responsible for taxes on funds you receive. FanZuP or its payment partner will collect your tax information and issue any required information return (such as Form 1099-K). *(Brief Q7: confirm who is the payment settlement entity.)*

4.2 If your perks are taxable goods or tickets, sales tax will be handled as set out in [schedule]. *(Brief Q8 / FR-TAX-005: whether FanZuP is a marketplace facilitator; until then, attorney to advise who collects.)*

## 5. Delivering perks

5.1 You must deliver every perk as described, to every backer entitled to it, by the delivery date on your campaign.

5.2 **If a perk will be late,** you must give backers a new date within [7] days of the original date passing (FanZuP's app will prompt you), and backers can then cancel for a full refund. *(Brief Q6: if the FTC Mail Order Rule applies, the notice timing and consent rules there control.)*

5.3 **Tickets:** you're responsible for the event, the venue and admitting valid ticket codes. If an event is cancelled, backers holding tickets are entitled to a refund.

5.4 **Physical goods** must be safe, lawful and as described; you're the seller of record for them. *(Attorney: confirm.)*

5.5 **Problems:** respond to backer problem reports in the app within [x] business days.

## 6. Backer data

6.1 You'll receive only the information needed to deliver each perk (for example name and shipping address for a physical perk, after funding). You'll receive a backer's email only if they opt in to hear from you directly.

6.2 **You may use backer information only to deliver that campaign's perks and to communicate about that campaign.** You may not add backers to mailing lists, sell, rent or share their information, or use it for any other purpose, unless the backer separately agrees. Exports carry this notice.

6.3 Keep backer information secure, don't keep it longer than you need for delivery and your records, and tell FanZuP at once if it's lost or misused. *(Attorney: add state-law contractor/processor terms as needed.)*

## 7. Content and rights

7.1 You must own or have licences for all music, lyrics, artwork, video, names and likenesses in your campaign and perks, including any samples, covers (mechanical licences) and collaborators' contributions.

7.2 You grant FanZuP the content licence in the Terms of Service, including the right to feature your campaign in FanZuP's promotion.

## 8. Suspension and cancellation
FanZuP may suspend a campaign that looks fraudulent, misleading, infringing or broken. While it's suspended no money moves in or out. FanZuP may cancel a campaign and refund all backers if it finds fraud, a material misrepresentation, a breach of this agreement or a legal requirement. You must repay any released funds needed to refund backers in that case. *(Attorney: confirm enforceability and collection approach.)*

## 9. Updates and honesty with backers
Post updates when milestones are reached or delayed, and tell backers promptly about anything that materially affects the project or their perks.

## 10. Liability and indemnity
You'll indemnify FanZuP against third-party claims (including backers' claims) arising from your campaign, your perks, your content or your breach of this agreement. FanZuP's liability to you is limited as set out in the Terms of Service. *(Attorney: consider a cap tied to fees FanZuP received from the campaign.)*

## 11. Term and changes
This agreement applies to each campaign until all perks are delivered, all funds are released or refunded and all disputes are closed. FanZuP may update this agreement with [30] days' notice; changes don't apply to a campaign already live unless the law requires.
