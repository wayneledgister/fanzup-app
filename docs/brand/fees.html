Fee Structure
FanzUp's revenue comes from platform fees on investment campaigns, subscription revenue shares, tip processing, merch commission, and affiliate referral fees. Most fees are still TBD pending product team, legal, and finance alignment. The one known fee is payment processing pass-through.

⚖️
Reg CF Disclosure Requirement: All Fees Must Be in the Form C

Under Rule 203(b)(1)(v) of Regulation Crowdfunding, all compensation paid to or charged by the intermediary (FanzUp and its broker-dealer partner) must be disclosed in the Form C. Platform fees, broker-dealer intermediary fees, and any other compensation must be clearly and accurately stated before any campaign goes live. Undisclosed fees are a material regulatory violation.

Campaign Platform Fee

Charged as a percentage of the total successfully funded campaign amount. Applies only to campaigns that reach their funding target — not to failed or partially-funded campaigns. This is FanzUp's primary revenue from investment products.

  • → Applied to funds released from escrow at campaign close
  • → Must be disclosed in Form C before campaign launch
  • → Industry range: Republic and Wefunder charge 5–8% on funds raised
  • → Broker-dealer also charges its own intermediary fee (separate line item)

TBD% of revenue

Subscription Revenue Share

FanzUp retains a percentage of all subscription revenue collected on behalf of artists. Applies to all tiers. Artist receives the remainder net of payment processing.

  • → Applied monthly to all active subscription payments
  • → Comparable platforms: Patreon 5–12%, Substack 10%
  • → Lower rate may be appropriate to incentivize supply-side growth early
  • → Not subject to Form C disclosure (not an investment product)

Tip / Buy-a-Coffee Processing Fee

A processing fee applied to tip and donation transactions. Covers platform operating costs beyond the pass-through payment processing fee. May be covered entirely by the payment processing pass-through at low tip amounts.

  • → Comparable: Ko-fi takes 0% (free), Buy Me a Coffee takes 5%
  • → Consider waiving entirely at T1 to reduce friction for new artist revenue
  • → Not subject to Form C disclosure

TBD% commission

Merch Commission

Commission on native FanzUp merch listings only. Link-out to external Shopify or third-party stores is commission-free — FanzUp earns revenue only on sales processed through the FanzUp native checkout.

  • → Printful fulfillment costs passed through at cost; FanzUp adds platform commission on top
  • → Link-outs to Shopify and external stores: no commission collected
  • → Not subject to Form C disclosure (not an investment product)
💳
~3.2%pass-through

Payment Processing Pass-Through

Stripe processing fees passed through to artists at cost — not a FanzUp revenue item. Applies to all payment transactions: subscriptions, tips, merch, ticket sales, and investment transfers.

  • → Stripe standard rate: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
  • → International cards: additional 1.5% (if US-only platform, may be rare)
  • → Apple Pay / Google Pay: same rates as cards via Stripe
  • → FanzUp may negotiate volume-discounted rates with Stripe as platform scales

TBDreferral rate

Affiliate Referral Fee

Fees paid out to affiliate partners (managers, agents, marketing agencies) who refer successful artists or investing fans to FanzUp. This is a cost to FanzUp, not revenue from artists or fans — but must be disclosed in Form C if referral arrangements are in place for a campaign.

  • → Affiliate earns fee on successful artist onboarding OR on fan investment conversion
  • → Different rates per affiliate tier may apply (standard vs. premium partner)
  • → Must be disclosed in Form C per Rule 203(b)(1)(v) — referral compensation is material

Pricing Strategy Consideration

FanzUp occupies a unique position: the only platform combining subscription content (Patreon-like) with SEC Reg CF investment campaigns (Republic-like). This justifies a premium fee on the investment side (Reg CF compliance is expensive) but a competitive or below-market fee on subscriptions and tips to win supply-side market share from Patreon and Bandcamp. The exact rate structure should be modeled by finance across multiple growth scenarios before the product team commits to any rates that must be disclosed in Form C.

💡
Broker-Dealer Partner Fees

FanzUp's FINRA-registered broker-dealer partner charges its own intermediary fees for each campaign — separate from the FanzUp platform fee. These fees are negotiated as part of the broker-dealer partnership agreement and must also be disclosed in each campaign's Form C. The total cost to the artist for running a Reg CF campaign will be FanzUp platform fee + broker-dealer fee + payment processing. Finance must model artist net proceeds accordingly.
