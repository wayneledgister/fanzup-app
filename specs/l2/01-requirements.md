## Demo posture (applies to every requirement below)
- **D-1** Everything is behind the `layer2` flag, enforced by the API (404 `layer2_disabled` on every Layer 2 fan and creator route and on flag-dependent staff routes) and by the database. **What the flag gates (PR #10 review round 3, finding 4):** only **entry paths** — anything that starts new activity: Pool submit, review, collection execution and launch; reserve/invest; tranche evidence and verification; statement upload; distribution dry-run/commit; the mock deposit trigger and other flag-dependent staff actions. **Completion paths run whatever the flag says**, so money already in flight always finishes and investors keep their rights: **investor cancellation of an existing commitment** (the 17 CFR 227.304(a) right to cancel until 48 hours before the deadline holds with the flag off; PR #10 review round 5, finding 3), staff collection-state moves of a Pool already in default (round 5, finding 4), fund-move, refund, disbursement, deposit and payout confirmations (webhooks), reservation expiry, settlement at the deadline (target-or-refund), maturity, the outbound-op runner, revenue reconciliation and Layer 2 recon; staff keep the Pool money view, queue and recon. Turning the flag **off is always allowed**, even with escrow, collection cash or open operations; it stops new activity and lets in-flight money complete (design §2). While the flag is off, revenue reconciliation makes no adverse collection-state transition (to AT_RISK or DEFAULT) — it still reconciles cash and still cures — so no new default arises while creators can't upload statements (round 5, finding 4). The flag is off by default and can't be on in a deployed environment: Layer 2 requires the mock Reg CF provider, and the API refuses to start with the mock provider when deployed.
- **D-2** Every Layer 2 screen shows a persistent banner: "Demo — not an offer of securities. Mock escrow and data." **Every Layer 2 notice and the mock Form C carry the same sentence** (PR #10 review round 5, finding 6); `pnpm lint:copy` fails if a Layer 2 notice template or the Form C lacks it.
- **D-3** No real money, KYC, filings or tax forms. Stripe is never used for Layer 2.
- **D-4** Copy follows Brand §7.4: payouts are always "potential" and paired with risk; the risk disclosure precedes any purchase; Form C link, Reg CF limit, 12-month lock-up and **the intermediary (funding portal or broker-dealer) through which the offering is made** are shown — until one is chosen the intermediary is shown as **"Intermediary: pending — L2-Q3"**, and no copy says or implies that a registered intermediary is already in place (PR #10 review, finding 7); nothing implies liquidity; nothing is sorted or ranked by money or return (PRD 02 §8). Product nouns: Pools, Units, Waterfall. `pnpm lint:copy` covers Layer 2 screens and Layer 2 notices.

## 1. Creator: album royalty Pool (FR-L2-CR)

### FR-L2-CR-001: Only Rising and above can create a Pool
**Story:** As a creator at Rising tier or above, I want a separate path to offer fans a share of an album's royalties, so that Layer 2 stays out of the reward-campaign flow.
- Given a creator whose tier is Starter, when they open or call Pool creation, then they're refused with the tier rule (PRD 01 §6.3: Starter has no Reg CF access) and shown what Rising requires.
- The tier is read from `packages/shared` tiers and recorded on the Pool at submission (assessed at submission, not mid-offering).
- **Issuer cap (PR #10 review, finding 1).** The Pool's **maximum** (Units × Unit price — not its target, since sales can run past the target up to the maximum) can't exceed the creator's **remaining 12-month Reg CF cap**: the tier cap (never above `POLICY.regCf.issuerCapMinor`, 17 CFR 227.100(a)(1)) minus, over the creator's other Reg CF offerings, the amount raised by those that closed in the trailing 12 months and the **maximum** of those still in review, approved or live. Checked at **submission** and again at **launch** (another Pool may have been submitted or launched in between), both under a lock on **every artist row of the Pool's owner** (taken in id order) so two Pools can't pass the check concurrently. **The cap aggregates by owner, not by artist profile (PR #10 review round 4, finding 5):** one owner can hold several artist profiles, and they are one issuer for the 12-month cap, so "the creator's other Reg CF offerings" means every Pool of every artist profile with the same owner; refused with `tier_cap_exceeded`. The remaining cap at launch is recorded on the Pool, and `reserve_investment` refuses any reservation that would take the Pool's committed amount past it (`issuer_cap_reached`) — a defensive invariant (with the launch check it can't refuse anything today; it guards future changes to the cap or the maximum; round 3, finding 10).
- **Sales outside FanZuP count (PR #10 review round 3, finding 5).** The 12-month cap aggregates every Reg CF (§4(a)(6)) sale by the issuer in the preceding 12 months (17 CFR 227.100(a)(1)), and "issuer" includes entities controlled by or under common control with it and its predecessors (227.100(c)); other exempt, non-crowdfunding offerings don't count. The creator attests the amount of such sales made outside FanZuP (self-reported, labelled "pending counsel") at **submission** and again at **launch**; the attested figure is subtracted from the remaining cap in both checks and in the reservation backstop. Who counts as the issuer and whether an attestation suffices is counsel item **L2-Q12** (with L2-Q2); the demo already treats all artist profiles of one owner as one issuer (round 4, finding 5).

### FR-L2-CR-002: Album basics, story, use of funds, milestones
- Album title, artist display name, planned release date, tracklist (1–30 tracks), artwork (rendered with `ArtistArt`; no uploads in the demo), story, risks.
- Use of funds as line items; the **funding target equals the sum of line items**. Maximum = Units × Unit price, and must be ≥ target.
- Production milestones: 2–3 tranches whose percentages sum to 100; tranche 1 releases at close; later tranches need evidence and staff verification (same rules as M1 milestones).

### FR-L2-CR-003: Royalty terms
- Revenue types the Pool participates in: **master recording royalties** (default, on), sync (optional), publishing/PRO (selectable but labelled "Trust-based — pending counsel"; Mechanism 01 revenue-type table).
- Split of covered royalties across **fans / creator / platform** in basis points; must sum to 100%. The platform share is a placeholder labelled "pending fee schedule" (fees are TBD per `docs/brand/fees.html`).
- Number of Units, Unit price, minimum Units per investor, deadline (**21–60 days**; the minimum is policy `regCf.minOfferingDays = 21` — Reg CF requires the offering information to be public for at least 21 days before any securities are sold, 17 CFR 227.303(a)(2), with no transmission of funds to the issuer earlier than that, 227.303(e)(3)(i), and an early close only after at least 21 days, 227.304(b)(1); confirmation is counsel item L2-Q10; PR #10 review, finding 2), **return cap multiple (default 1.5×)**, **maturity (default 5 years)**, distribution schedule (default quarterly).
- The creator sees a plain-language summary and a potential-payout illustration that always includes the zero case.

### FR-L2-CR-004: Collection mechanism and risk badge
- Mechanism (01b §1.1 enum + SPLIT_PAYEE per Mechanism 01 integration update): `DISTRIBUTOR_REDIRECT`, `SPLIT_PAYEE`, `LOCKBOX`, `SELF_REPORT`, `LETTER_OF_DIRECTION`.
- Each revenue type caps the strongest mechanism allowed for it; the effective tier is the weakest across the Pool's revenue types × mechanism. Derived **risk badge**: `SECURED_ISH`, `VERIFIED`, `TRUST_BASED` (internal names, never shown). Shown labels: **"More secure collection"**, "Verified collection", "Trust-based collection". Wherever the strongest badge is shown to a fan, the separate line **"Your claim is unsecured"** appears next to it (gate condition 5; one wording everywhere — design §8, gate record; PR #10 review, finding 3).
- **01b AC-R6:** a Pool can't go live until its collection-mechanism record is **executed** (by staff, after the creator submits it).

### FR-L2-CR-005: Submit → Form C review → launch
- Submit generates a **mock Form C** document from the Pool's data (issuer, offering terms, use of funds, risk factors, collection mechanism, tax characterization note), stored with its SHA-256 hash.
- Staff review: approve or request revisions (reason required). Reviewer ≠ owner; single-operator rules (FR-ID-007) apply.
- Launch (creator, after approval and executed collection mechanism): the provider issuer and offering are created in the mock escrow service; the deadline clock starts.

## 2. Fan: invest (FR-L2-INV)

### FR-L2-INV-001: Discovery
- `/pools` lists live Pools; filters by revenue type, risk badge and genre; ordered by deadline (soonest first) or newest; **never** by money raised, Units sold or anything return-like.
- `/pools/:id`: album, terms in plain language, risk badge with explanation, illiquidity / principal-at-risk / **unsecured claim** disclosures, Form C link, lock-up, maturity, potential-payout illustration that includes zero.

### FR-L2-INV-002: Investor onboarding
- Mock KYC/AML through the provider party (name + state); result arrives asynchronously by webhook: `approved`, `rejected`, or `manual_review` (staff decides).
- Income, net worth and accredited-status attestation; Reg CF investments made elsewhere in the last 12 months (self-reported).
- State eligibility: a demo deny-list (policy `l2.blockedStates`, empty by default) is checked before purchase.

### FR-L2-INV-003: Reg CF investment limit (01a §3)
- Limit per 17 CFR 227.100(a)(2) (policy `regCf.investorLimits`, with source and date verified): non-accredited → if annual income **or** net worth is below the threshold, the greater of the floor amount or the lower percentage of the greater of income/net worth; if both are at or above it, the higher percentage of the greater, capped at the threshold. Accredited → no limit.
- **Counted reservation (PR #10 review round 4, finding 1 — one definition, used everywhere):** an investment with status `reserved` **and** either unexpired (`reserve_expires_at > now`) **or** with a provider call started (`provider_call_started_at` set). A reservation with a provider call started may have moved money at the provider, so it stays counted — past its expiry, during the expiry-lookup grace, while its fund move is pending — until the expiry/cancel resolution (design §4.3) moves it to a terminal state (`expired`, `cancelled`, `returned`) or another counted state (`refund_pending`). Nothing else takes it out of the count — settlement at the deadline doesn't either: such a reservation is never issued, stays counted for the investor's limit and the Pool's Units until the resolution, and any money it moved is refunded in full (PR #10 review round 5, finding 1; design §5).
- Usage = all of the investor's active and completed Reg CF commitments on FanZuP in the trailing 12 months (**counted reservations**, `funding`, `funded`, `issued`, `refund_pending`) + self-reported elsewhere. A `refund_pending` investment keeps counting until its refund is confirmed (same list as design §3; PR #10 review round 2, finding 10).
- **01a AC-I1:** concurrent purchases by the same investor across any offerings can never together exceed the limit (exactly the ones that fit succeed).
- **01a AC-I2:** a reservation that isn't funded expires and frees its limit; a returned fund move frees it immediately. **A reservation for which a provider call was started stays a counted reservation (above) and frees its limit only after the provider confirms there is no trade or the trade is voided; if a fund move already exists it stays counted until that fund move is refunded or returned** — so a crash after the fund move followed by expiry can never let the investor reserve past the limit (round 4, finding 1). No fund move is ever started for an expired reservation or a Pool that is no longer live** (PR #10 review round 3, finding 1; design §4.3, §5).
- **01a AC-I5:** limits are computed and enforced transactionally in the database, never only in the client; the API returns the remaining headroom.

### FR-L2-INV-004: Purchase
- Order: risk acknowledgment (versioned) → Units reserved (limit + availability) → provider trade → fund move into the offering escrow → confirmation page. Idempotent per `Idempotency-Key`.
- Units can't oversell the Pool; self-investment by the creator is refused.
- Cancellation allowed until 48 hours before the deadline (17 CFR 227.304(a); full refund through the provider). **The cut-off is judged when the investor asks, never when money arrives** (PR #10 review round 2, finding 1 — replaces the round-1 rule that refused cancellation while a fund move was in flight, which could deny the right to cancel). A `reserved` investment **for which no provider call was made** is cancelled at once ("nothing was charged"). **A `reserved` investment with a provider call started (PR #10 review round 4, finding 2) may already have money moving at the provider, so its cancellation is accepted and recorded (`cancel_requested_at`, result `cancel_pending`, "Cancellation received — if any payment was taken it will be refunded in full") and resolved by the provider lookup (design §4.3): no trade, or the trade voided → `cancelled` with a notice that nothing was charged; a fund move exists → when it settles, `refund_pending` and a refund of the received amount with a notice stating that amount; returned → `returned`. "Nothing was charged" is said only when no provider call was made or the provider confirmed there is no trade or a voided one.** A `funded` one moves to `refund_pending` and is refunded in full. An investment in `funding` (fund move in flight) is **accepted and recorded at request time** (`cancel_requested_at`; shown as "Cancellation received — we'll refund your payment in full as soon as it arrives"): when the fund move settles — before or after the cut-off, before or after the deadline — the investment goes straight to `refund_pending` and the received amount is refunded in full; if the fund move is returned instead, the investment simply closes as `returned` (no money arrived, nothing to refund). A cancellation-pending investment is never issued at close.

- **Notice of investment commitment (17 CFR 227.303(d); PR #10 review round 4, finding 6; rule text checked 2026-10-11 at https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/227.303):** promptly on receipt of the commitment — when the fund move starts (`mark_investment_funding` → `funding`), or when the fund move settles first and is applied to the reservation — the investor gets **one** notice (never two for one investment; round 5, finding 2), and the confirmation page shows the same fields: (1) the dollar amount of the commitment; (2) the Unit price and number of Units; (3) the issuer's name (the demo shows the artist's issuer name, labelled pending L2-Q2); (4) the date and time (with time zone) by which the investor may cancel — `ends_at` − 48 hours.
- **Transaction confirmation (17 CFR 227.303(f)(1)):** at or before completion — in the `settle_pool` funded branch that issues the Units — each investor gets a confirmation with: (i) the date of the transaction (close date); (ii) the type of security ("revenue-share Units", pending counsel L2-Q2); (iii) the identity, price and number of Units the investor bought, and the number of Units the issuer sold in the offering and their price; (iv) the interest rate and yield to maturity **if** the Units are a debt security — the demo states the return cap and maturity date instead and marks the item pending counsel (L2-Q9); (v) the first call date if callable — Pool terms have no call, shown as "not callable"; (vi) the source, form and amount of any remuneration received in connection with the transaction — the platform's royalty share ("pending fee schedule", L2-Q7) and intermediary compensation ("pending — L2-Q3"). Items (iv) and (vi) are shown only partially until counsel and the intermediary answer (L2-Q9).

### FR-L2-INV-005: Portfolio
- Units held, amount, status, lock-up end date and countdown (12 months from issue; Mechanism 07 P0: no transfers, no prices), potential vs received distributions, return-cap progress, maturity date, documents (Form C, confirmation, distribution statements).

### FR-L2-INV-006: Cancellation and failed-offering notices; refund timing (PR #10 review round 3, finding 6)
- 17 CFR 227.304(d): if an offering isn't completed, within five business days the intermediary notifies each investor of the cancellation, the reason and the refund amount expected, directs the refund, and stops new commitments; 227.303(e)(3)(ii)–(iii) return funds on a cancelled commitment or an uncompleted offering; 227.304(c) applies the same five-business-day rule to commitments cancelled for lack of reconfirmation (not built; gate condition 10). In production this is the intermediary's job (L2-Q3, L2-Q9); **the demo does it itself through the Layer 2 notices path**:
- **Failed Pool:** at settlement every investor gets a notice with the cancellation, the reason (target not reached by the deadline) and their refund amount (what actually arrived), and every refund is directed in the same step; the Pool stops taking reservations at once.
- **Investor cancellation:** the investor gets a cancellation notice with the reason and the refund amount ("nothing was charged" only for a reservation with no provider call, or once the provider confirms no trade / a voided trade; "refund in full as soon as it arrives" for a cancellation pending on a provider call or fund move, then a notice with the amount when it arrives; round 4, finding 2).
- **Fund move that can't be applied:** the investor gets a notice with the reason and the refund amount.
- **Timing:** the refund is directed when it becomes owed (same transaction); a refund not yet sent to the provider within `l2.refundDirectBusinessDays` (5) business days raises a staff-visible `refund_overdue` break and alert.

## 3. Escrow and money after funding (FR-L2-ESC, FR-L2-REV, FR-L2-WF)

### FR-L2-ESC-001: Escrow invariants (01a AC-E1…E5)
- **AC-E1** (reworded, PR #10 review, finding 5; round 2, finding 3) **Before close:** Σ **received amounts** (`funded_received_minor`, what the fund move actually delivered) of the offering's `funded` + `refund_pending` investments + Σ open stray fund moves (received, refund not yet confirmed; design §3.1) == the escrow FBO ledger account (`pool_escrow`), after every committed money function. **Ledger vs provider (any time):** the escrow FBO ledger account == the provider's escrow balance for the offering when compared after the event inbox is drained; when compared with events or outbound operations still in flight, the in-flight amounts are reported separately and don't count as a difference (the M1 in-flight allowance, M1 design §7). After close the first equality no longer applies (money leaves by tranche); AC-E4/E5 govern it.
- **Fund moves we can't apply** (wrong amount, reservation expired or cancelled, Pool no longer live, a second fund move for an investment that already has one): the money is still recorded as received and refunded in full from what actually arrived — never from the expected amount — by its own refund; it never changes an applied investment (design §3.1).
- **AC-E2** Target-or-refund: at the deadline, funded ≥ target → close; else every investor is refunded in full. A reservation for which a provider call was started is neither issued nor counted toward the target; it stays a counted reservation until the provider lookup resolves it, and any money it moved is refunded in full (round 5, finding 1). A failed Pool is `refunded` only once nothing is left to refund or resolve (no refund pending, no payment in flight, no counted reservation, no open stray fund move; round 5, finding 7).
- **AC-E3** Nothing leaves escrow to the issuer before close.
- **AC-E4** After close, money is released only per verified milestone tranches (tranche 1 at close), never more than raised.
- **AC-E5** The escrow FBO account never goes negative and ends at exactly zero once every tranche is released or every investor refunded.

### FR-L2-REV-001: Royalty ingestion (01b §3)
- A royalty statement can be uploaded per period by the creator; because the creator uploads it, it is labelled **`SELF_REPORTED`** (01b vocabulary) whatever the collection mechanism. `API_VERIFIED` is reserved for statements delivered by a distributor's API, which the demo doesn't build (PR #10 review round 2, finding 9). The label is informational: only bank-reconciled cash is ever collected (below). A mock lockbox/collection-account settlement (verification `BANK_RECONCILED`) arrives through the provider.
- A reconciliation run matches statements to settlements. **Only bank-reconciled cash becomes `collected`** and distributable (**01b AC-R1, R2**). A statement without cash is "reported, not collected" (**AC-R3**). A settlement short of its statement is collected at the cash amount and opens a shortfall break (**AC-R4**). Reconciliation is idempotent and re-runnable (**AC-R8**).

### FR-L2-REV-002: Default handling (01b §5)
- Collection state machine `COLLECTING → AT_RISK → DEFAULT → REMEDIATION → COLLECTING | CHARGED_OFF`. A period whose settlement is missing or short past its due date moves to AT_RISK; uncured after the cure period moves to DEFAULT; staff move DEFAULT to REMEDIATION or CHARGED_OFF with a reason. **REMEDIATION → COLLECTING is a staff action, never automatic** (PR #10 review, finding 4): allowed only once the Pool is cured — bank-reconciled cash covers every missed period, so no period is past due — and refused otherwise (`not_cured`); it goes through `privileged()` with a typed reason and is audited. REMEDIATION → CHARGED_OFF is also a staff action. (AT_RISK → COLLECTING stays automatic when the late cash is reconciled before DEFAULT.) Every transition notifies the Pool's holders.

### FR-L2-WF-001: Waterfall (01a §4)
- Distribution runs are **deterministic and replayable**: dry-run and commit produce the same allocation and the same hash for the same inputs (**AC-W1**).
- Pro-rata by Units; each investment's cumulative distributions never exceed its return cap; once the fan class reaches the cap, the rest of the fan share goes to the creator (**AC-W2**).
- Only collected, unallocated revenue is distributed (**AC-W3**).
- Conservation to the cent: fans + creator + platform == collected amount of the run, with the documented rounding rule (largest remainder by Units, ties by investment id) (**AC-W4**).
- Commit is idempotent: committing the same run twice moves money once (**AC-W6**).
- **Maturity (PR #10 review round 2, finding 8 — one definition):** maturity is a **date**, `matures_at` = close + the Pool's maturity term. The worker moves a `funded` Pool to `matured` (`matured_reason = 'maturity'`) at `matures_at`, whether or not a distribution runs. Expected royalty periods stop at maturity (the last one ends at `matures_at`), so no period after it can be missed. After maturity, cash for periods ending on or before `matures_at` can still be reconciled and **one or more final distribution runs** can be committed on the `matured` Pool (same Waterfall and caps); statements for periods after maturity are refused (`pool_matured`). Separately, a commit after which every issued investment is at its cap also ends the Pool (`matured_reason = 'cap_reached'`).
- Payouts go through the provider; each payout records its tax characterization (default **1099-DIV**, C-corp-taxed issuer, Mechanism 04 — counsel item) and contributes to a per-investor, per-year 1099 data row. Data only; no forms.
