# E1 Council Review — PRAGMATIST seat

Lens: effort, scope, shipping reality. Date: 2026-10-03.

## Findings

**Bottom line:** the repo is a good-looking 93-route prototype plus a small, well-built money core: 8 API endpoints and 3 migrations. Nothing connects the two yet. "Enterprise level" for a pre-launch, solo-founder product isn't more screens. It means one flow that is real end to end: an artist launches a Fund My Show campaign, fans back it, money sits with a custodian, and the campaign either releases to the artist or refunds everyone. That flow should be observable, auditable and recoverable. Today 0 of 93 routes do that.

## Findings

### P1 [BLOCKER]: None of the 93 routes uses the real API; the "app" is a prototype
- **Target:** `apps/web/src/lib/api.ts` (its own header says "Not wired into pages yet"); 31 files import `src/lib/mock.ts`; `specs/backlog.md` row "Wire the web app to the API" (M).
- **What's missing:**
  - No page calls `api.campaigns()`, `api.campaign()` or `api.back()`.
  - There's no Supabase client in `apps/web/package.json`. The `LogIn`/`SignUp` pages (`src/pages/auth/*`, `components/public/auth.tsx`) are form UI with no auth behind them.
- **Why it matters:** every "enterprise" conversation about this product is about a demo until the backing flow runs from the browser.
- **Fix:** before any new feature, wire four screens end to end: `/explore`, `/campaigns/:id`, the back/checkout step and `fan/BackedAndPerks`. Add Supabase Auth sign-in and Stripe Elements on the `clientSecret` that `POST /v1/backings` already returns.
- **Size:** M (3–5 focused evenings). That's the realistic estimate. The work itself is small. The time goes into the auth session plumbing and loading/error states on four pages.

### P2 [BLOCKER]: The API can take money but can't create the thing money goes to
- **Target:** `apps/api/src/routes/*`, which has 8 endpoints and none to create an artist, a campaign, perks or tranches.
- **The database is ahead of the API:** `supabase/migrations/20261003000300_workflow_and_rls.sql` already defines `submit_campaign`, `review_campaign`, `publish_campaign` and `verify_tranche`. No route calls them.
- **The web side is fully mocked:** the 26-route artist/campaign wizard (`pages/artist/*`, `pages/campaign/*`) and the 5 admin pages (`pages/admin/*`) persist nothing. Today the only campaigns that can exist come from `supabase/seed.sql`.
- **Fix:** add these API routes, all thin wrappers over SQL that already exists:
  - `POST/PATCH /v1/campaigns` (draft)
  - `PUT /v1/campaigns/:id/perks`
  - `POST /v1/campaigns/:id/submit`
  - `POST /v1/admin/campaigns/:id/review`
  - `POST /v1/admin/campaigns/:id/publish`
  - `POST /v1/tranches/:id/evidence` and `POST /v1/tranches/:id/verify`
- **Size:** M.

### P3 [BLOCKER]: Escrow and custody is the critical path, and it's the slowest dependency
- **Target:**
  - `apps/api/src/escrow/provider.ts`: "partner isn't chosen yet".
  - `apps/api/src/escrow/stripe-dev.ts`: "Stripe states it does not provide escrow services — this is NOT the production custody model".
  - `docs/SETUP.md` Part G: "6–12 months".
- **The problem:** the README's first money rule is "FanZuP never holds cash". Until a partner signs, no real reward campaign can launch on the advertised promise.
- **This item will take about 3x longer than it looks:**
  - The adapter file is "one file" (SETUP Part G).
  - Around it sit vendor diligence, underwriting, the money-transmission posture, an FBO sub-ledger reconciliation design, and the partner's sandbox quirks (async webhooks, return codes, ACH vs card timing).
  - North Capital may decline non-securities reward escrow. That's the first question in Part G, and the answer could invalidate the plan.
- **Fix:** send the Part G questions **this week**; it's calendar time, not engineering time. In parallel, write down the fallback launch posture so the product can ship without waiting 6–12 months. For example, a payment-facilitator model with funds held by the processor and refunds on failure, with copy that doesn't say "escrow" until a custodian exists. That posture needs a counsel opinion, and that opinion is the actual unblocker.
- **Size:** engineering M. Calendar time L–XL.

### P4 [CONCERN]: 5,083 lines of unused shadcn/ui and about 20 dependencies that ship nothing
- **Target:** `apps/web/src/components/ui/*`.
  - Nothing outside `ui/` imports any of its 46 files.
  - Within `ui/`, files import only each other (e.g. `sidebar` imports `button`/`sheet`/`input`).
- **The dependencies behind it (verified by grep outside `ui/`):** `apps/web/package.json` carries 26 `@radix-ui/*` packages. Of these, only `react-slot` (`brand/Button.tsx`) and `react-dialog` (`fan/kit.tsx`) are used. `recharts` is used by `creator/Revenue.tsx` and `creator/Dashboard.tsx`, so keep it. These have **zero** uses outside `ui/`: `react-hook-form`, `cmdk`, `embla-carousel-react`, `input-otp`, `react-day-picker`, `react-resizable-panels`, `next-themes`, `vaul`, `sonner`, `date-fns`. `BUILD_CONVENTIONS.md` says to compose from `@/components/brand`, so the shadcn layer is a dead parallel design system.
- **Why it matters:** this is ceremony. Dependabot (`.github/dependabot.yml`) will open PRs for packages nobody uses, and new contributors (or agents) will reach for `ui/button` instead of `brand/Button`.
- **Fix:** delete `components/ui/` and prune the unused dependencies. Verify with `pnpm typecheck && pnpm build`. Re-add a single Radix primitive when a brand component needs it (Dialog, Tooltip, Select are the likely ones).
- **Size:** S (about 1 hour).

### P5 [CONCERN]: 38% of the routes are gated securities surfaces with no backend
- **Target:** `routes.manifest.json` has 22 `layer2` routes and 2 `postBeta` routes (`pages/invest/*`, the 12 `wizard[layer2]` KYC/investor routes, `kyc/*`).
- **Why they can't go further now:** `specs/backlog.md` sizes the Layer 2 schema as L, and the D1 Skeptic note says all of it sits on the unrun reward-vs-profit-share A/B test.
- **The real gate is outside the code:** Layer 2 needs a FINRA funding portal or broker-dealer partner (`flags.ts` header: "Off until partner funding portal / BD + counsel sign-off").
  - This is the canonical "looks like a flag flip, is actually 6–18 months": partner contract, Form C preparation per issuer, transfer agent (Mechanism 04), and investor-limit computation done by the intermediary.
  - None of it is code a solo founder controls.
- **Fix:** freeze all `layer2`/`postBeta` UI at its current quality. Don't polish it, add to it or wire it. Keep it reachable only via `?flags=` for investor and partner demos.
- **Size:** 0 (it's a decision).

### P7 [CONCERN]: Mock data is spread across 5+ sources; plan the swap-out or wiring will drift
- **Target:**
  - `src/lib/mock.ts` (225 lines)
  - `components/creator/data.ts` (264 lines)
  - `components/campaign/reviewData.ts` (281 lines)
  - `components/campaign/identityData.ts`
  - `components/invest/data.ts`
  - `components/fan/live-data.ts`
- **The trap:** `BUILD_CONVENTIONS.md` § Data tells builders to "prefer local constants in your page". That made sense for parallel prototyping. Wiring will now go page by page, and each page invents its own fetch and loading pattern.
- **Fix:**
  1. Introduce one data-access seam: `src/lib/queries.ts` with TanStack Query hooks (`useCampaigns`, `useCampaign`, `useMyBackings`). Each hook returns mock data or API data based on a single env switch.
  2. Wire pages to the hooks, not to `api.ts` directly. This lets the prototype keep working for unwired areas, and wired areas flip over one at a time.
  3. Update BUILD_CONVENTIONS § Data to say this.
- **Size:** S to set up. It then saves time on every page.

### P8 [CONCERN]: No production-readiness basics, and these are the enterprise items worth doing now
- **Targets:**
  - `apps/api/src/app.ts`: pino logger with redaction is good, but there's no error tracking, no request ID propagated to the worker, no metrics.
  - `apps/api/src/worker.ts`: errors are only `console.error`; a stuck outbox or failed refund alerts nobody.
  - There's no transactional email at all. Backers get no receipt, no "campaign funded" and no "you've been refunded". SETUP has no email provider part.
  - `specs/backlog.md` has a Playwright sweep "port into CI" (S) still open.
- **What's adequate already:** for a money product, "enterprise" means you find out about a failed refund before the fan does. A hosted dashboard isn't that. The ledger and idempotency work (`20261003000200_ledger_and_money.sql`, `routes/backings.ts`) is already at that bar.
- **What's missing:** the operational layer around it.
- **Fix:**
  - Sentry (or equivalent) on web, api and worker.
  - A worker heartbeat and an alert on an outbox item older than N minutes.
  - A nightly ledger-vs-processor reconciliation check.
  - Resend or Postmark for 4 transactional emails.
- **Size:** M in total. Each piece is S.

### P9 [CONCERN]: Identity verification UI exists, but no vendor integration or decision does
- **Target:**
  - `pages/artist/Kyc*.tsx` (9 routes)
  - `pages/kyc/*` (9 routes, `layer2`)
  - `core.sql` `artists.identity_status` / `identity_ref` ("Persona/Plaid")
- **Two distinct needs:**
  1. **Layer 1:** the artist being paid needs identity and payout-account verification. That's a payout and AML necessity, not a Reg CF one.
  2. **Layer 2:** investor KYC is done by the funding portal or broker-dealer partner. FanZuP shouldn't build its own.
- **The vendor work takes about 3x as long as it looks:** sandbox to production approval, webhook handling, manual-review queues and retention policy (Persona/Stripe Identity contracts and DPAs).
- **Fix:** for Layer 1, use the payout processor's built-in onboarding (Stripe Connect Express-style hosted KYC, or the escrow partner's own KYC). Don't integrate a separate KYC vendor. That collapses 9 artist KYC routes into one handoff screen plus a status. Investor KYC is out of scope.
- **Size:** S if processor-hosted. L if a standalone vendor.

### P10 [CONCERN]: The admin and ops surface is summary-only, and a solo founder *is* ops

  2. Tranche evidence verification.
  3. A read-only money view per campaign: ledger balance, backings, refunds and outbox status.
- **Leave as backlog:** the dispute queue until the first dispute.
- **Size:** M.

## What "enterprise level" should mean for FanZuP at this stage
Enterprise level here doesn't mean more screens, SSO or microservices. It means:
1. **Money correctness you can prove.** Most of this already exists: the double-entry ledger, idempotency, RLS, and CI against real Postgres. Add nightly reconciliation against the processor or custodian.
2. **One complete, real flow.** An artist creates a campaign, it's reviewed and published, fans back it with a real card, and the campaign either releases its tranches or refunds everyone. Every step is persisted and audited.
3. **Operability for one person:**
   - error tracking
   - alerts on stuck money
   - an admin money view
   - transactional email
   - backups with a tested restore
4. **Trust surfaces that are true.** Escrow copy is backed by a real custodian, or by an honest interim posture approved by counsel.
5. **Change discipline.** The spec, council and CR process is already in place. Keep it light enough that it doesn't eat the evenings.

Everything else ("highly desirable features") rides on top of that, and only once real campaigns exist to learn from.

## Feature recommendations
Ranked by fan and artist desirability per unit of solo effort. All are Layer 1 and all are reward-safe under copy rules.

| # | Feature | Value | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Shareable campaign page with live progress plus OG/social cards** (`/campaigns/:id` server-rendered meta and a dynamic OG image showing % funded) | Campaigns live or die on artists sharing to their own audience. This is the single biggest driver of backings, and it's cheap. | S |

| 5 | **Milestone release transparency timeline** (fan-visible tranche status: funded → evidence submitted → verified → released, from `campaign_tranches`) | Makes the escrow promise visible and is the trust differentiator against Kickstarter and GoFundMe. It's the "enterprise trust" feature fans actually see. | M |
| 6 | **Backer wall and engagement badges** (opt-in public backer list; badges count backings and attendance, never dollars, per D1 condition 3) | Social proof plus fan identity without ranking by money. Copy-safe. | S–M |

| 10 | **Ticket and QR redemption for show perks only** (a "GA ticket" perk issues a QR the artist scans at the door; no general ticketing) | The obvious payoff for "Fund My Show". It's scoped to campaign perks, not a ticketing platform. | M |
