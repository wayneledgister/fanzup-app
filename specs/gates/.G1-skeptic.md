# G1 Council Review: SKEPTIC seat

**Gate:** G1 (requirements) · **Artifacts:** `specs/00-brief.md`, `specs/01-requirements.md` · **Date:** 2026-10-03 · **Tier:** Enterprise
**Lens:** unvalidated assumptions about users, promises with no FR, measurability, hidden scope, nominal blocker coverage.

The requirements are thorough and well-sourced, and almost all of the sourcing is internal: E1 seat notes, repo docs, council D1. Not one requirement cites a fan, an artist, a staff member or a partner. The document also takes "staff" to be a team, while the brief says the staff is one person with a day job.

## What would change my mind
- The PRD trace is done (Q1), with each conflicting FR either changed or justified against doc precedence.
- One sentence in the requirements names the second human (an advisor, contractor or partner) who holds approval rights, or defines an audited single-operator mode with compensating controls, and states which P0s it relaxes until when.
- A custody FR exists that's written against a real counterparty. In the interim it can be Stripe-only reconciliation under card B option 1.
- An instrumentation P0 (funnel events + source capture), so that the brief's metrics have data from the first test-mode backing.
- Five artist conversations and one fan-checkout prototype test before the checkout P0s are frozen.

## Strongest reason not to proceed
These requirements answer E1's question ("what would a custodian, auditor and card network expect?") and never ask the brief's own users anything. Every source tag points inward, the PRDs that outrank this document haven't been read, and the operating model that most of the P0 controls depend on (separate reviewers, finance, compliance and an on-call backup) describes a company that doesn't exist. Approving now freezes about 37 P0 FRs and about 48 P0 NFRs. They put a compliance-heavy funnel in front of fans and artists nobody has tested it on, and they include controls that one person can satisfy only by pretending to be two. Meanwhile the one external fact everything depends on, the custody model, is still a switch that says "not yet". The cheaper move is to finish the PRD trace, write down the single-operator reality, and let a test-mode walking skeleton show where users actually drop off before this list becomes the contract for design.
