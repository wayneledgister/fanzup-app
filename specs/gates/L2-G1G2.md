## Dissent
- **Pragmatist vs Compliance/Ops on the default state machine.** Pragmatist: "Statement-based AT_RISK is enough for a demo; detecting missing periods is effort for an edge the demo never shows." Compliance/Ops: "A creator who simply stops sending statements is *the* default case in 01b §5; a state machine that only reacts to short statements misses it." Kept: missing-period detection, deterministic with `now` passed in (Pragmatist's simplicity condition applied to the implementation, not the rule).
- **Skeptic vs Architect on the seed reinterpretation.** Skeptic would rather add per-investor pricing to make "one fan at the cap" literally true; Architect: "Tiered pricing changes the offering terms and the Form C; don't invent terms to satisfy a fixture." Kept: Architect (condition 8).

## Skeptic's strongest reason not to proceed
This builds the most legally sensitive surface of the product before counsel has answered the questions that shape it (issuer entity/SPV, intermediary, 1099 characterization, publishing letters of direction). A polished demo creates pressure to ship what it shows. Mitigation accepted by the council: server-gated flag, can't run deployed, banner on every screen, and every counsel-dependent rule labelled in the UI and listed as L2-Q items.
