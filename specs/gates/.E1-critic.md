# E1 — Critic seat: experience & artifact quality review

Reviewer: CRITIC (independent). Lens: is FanZuP actually *good* for fans, artists and staff, or merely complete?

## Findings

### 1. [BLOCKER] The core fan journey has no "back" step: no checkout, no pledge confirmation, and the chosen perk is lost on sign-up
- **Target:** `apps/web/src/pages/campaigns/CampaignDetail.tsx:70` (`back()` → `/signup?next=…&perk=…`); `apps/web/src/pages/auth/SignUp.tsx:46` (navigates to `/verify-email?email=&role=` and drops `next` and `perk`); `apps/web/scripts/routes.manifest.json` (93 routes, none of them `/campaigns/:id/back`, `/checkout` or similar).
- **What happens:** a fan picks a perk and taps "Back with $25". They go to sign-up. `next` and `perk` are never read there (only `LogIn.tsx:28` reads `next`), so after verify-email → onboarding account/path/profile/discover/payment/complete they end up on `/home`. The campaign and perk they picked are gone. A fan who is already logged in is also sent to `/signup`, because `back()` doesn't check for a session.
- **Why it matters:** the whole product is "fan backs campaign". There is no screen that shows the perk, the amount, an optional tip on top of the perk, the shipping address for physical perks, the card, the escrow and refund promise right at the point of payment, or the confirmation ("You're backer #312, here's what happens next"). Kickstarter, Indiegogo and Patreon all treat this screen as their most-tested surface. Without it, 93 routes still don't deliver the one transaction the product exists for.
- **Fix:** add `/campaigns/:id/back?perk=` (perk summary, optional add-on amount, shipping address if `kind=physical`, saved card or new card, `<EscrowNotice/>`, "Authorized now, charged only if funded") and `/campaigns/:id/backed` (confirmation with share prompt and calendar of the end date). Carry `next`/`perk` through sign-up, verify and onboarding, or let a fan check out as a guest and create the account afterwards.

### 2. [BLOCKER] Campaigns can't be shared or found: one static `<title>`, no OG/Twitter cards, no per-route metadata, SPA-only rendering
- **Target:** `apps/web/index.html:6-11` (single `description`, `<title>FanZuP</title>`, no `og:*`, `twitter:*`, `canonical`); no `document.title` anywhere in `apps/web/src`; `vercel.json` rewrites every path to `index.html`, so crawlers get an empty `<div id="root">`.
- **Why it matters:** crowdfunding runs on sharing. Kickstarter says most pledges arrive from links outside the platform, shared by the artist and their fans. Today a link to `/campaigns/nova-live-band-tour` pasted into iMessage, X, Instagram bio, Discord or WhatsApp shows "FanZuP" with no image, title, goal or progress. Every browser tab is labeled "FanZuP", which also hurts screen-reader users and anyone with several tabs open. Search engines can't index artist or campaign pages. The product loses its main growth channel.
- **Fix:** add a per-route `<title>`/meta hook (react-router `meta` exports or a small `useDocumentMeta`). Serve campaign, artist and live pages with server-rendered or edge-injected OG tags and a dynamic OG image (artist art + title + "62% of $8,000 · 12 days left"). Add `canonical`, JSON-LD, `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`.

### 3. [CONCERN] "Share" is a silent clipboard write: no feedback, no fallback, no share sheet
- **Target:** `apps/web/src/pages/campaigns/CampaignDetail.tsx:121-129`.
- **Why it matters:** tapping Share gives no toast, no "Copied" state and no live region, so sighted users and screen-reader users alike can't tell anything happened. If `navigator.clipboard` is undefined (insecure context, some in-app browsers) nothing happens at all. On mobile the expected action is the native share sheet. The repo already has a working pattern (`components/creator/ui.tsx:220-251` with `aria-live` "Copied to clipboard") that this page ignores.
- **Fix:** use `navigator.share` when it exists and fall back to copy. Show visible feedback and announce it through a live region. Offer prefilled share text ("I just backed …") and a referral-tagged URL (see Highly desirable features).

### 4. [CONCERN] Notifications bell does nothing, so artists' and fans' feedback loops end in a dead button
- **Target:** `apps/web/src/layouts/shells.tsx:275-277` (`<Button aria-label="Notifications"><Bell/></Button>`, no `onClick`, no unread badge, no route); `apps/web/src/pages/settings/Settings.tsx:54-58` (five notification toggles for events that are never shown in-app).
- **Why it matters:** the most emotionally important moments in this product are "the campaign you backed got funded", "your perk shipped", "you got 3 new backers", "milestone released" and "admin requested revisions". The prototype has a preference page for these events but no place to see them. Staff, artists and fans all depend on this loop, and it's what Patreon, Kickstarter and Bandcamp use to bring people back.
- **Fix:** build a notification center (popover on desktop, `/notifications` page on mobile) with an unread count, grouping, mark-all-read and deep links. Add an empty state ("You're all caught up"). Use the same event taxonomy as the settings toggles.

### 5. [CONCERN] No reduced-motion handling anywhere; smooth scroll and entrance animations are unconditional
- **Target:** `apps/web/src/styles/index.css` (imports `tw-animate-css` at line 13; zero `prefers-reduced-motion` rules); `apps/web/src/pages/campaigns/CampaignDetail.tsx:118` (`scrollIntoView({ behavior: "smooth" })`, plus 3 more across pages); 19 `animate-*` uses in pages/components (`components/fan/kit.tsx:36` modal fade etc.); `components/brand/primitives.tsx:109` progress `transition-[width] duration-500`. `grep motion-reduce|prefers-reduced` returns 0 hits.
- **Why it matters:** WCAG 2.3.3 / 2.2.2. The brand also uses large animated blurred glows and pulsing live indicators. For an "enterprise-level" claim this is a basic gap that procurement accessibility audits (VPAT) catch.
- **Fix:** add a global `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` block that disables animations, transitions and smooth scroll. Use `motion-safe:` variants on pulses.

### 6. [BLOCKER] The public campaign page shows invented, identical "Where the money goes" and Updates on every campaign, which undermines the trust the product sells
- **Target:** `apps/web/src/pages/campaigns/CampaignDetail.tsx:268-273` (hard-coded 50/25/15/10 split applied to every goal); `:277` (story body is the *artist bio*, not the campaign story); `:324-327` (the same two updates, "Horn section locked in", rendered for every campaign, including a vinyl LP or documentary); `apps/web/src/lib/mock.ts:33-46` (the `Campaign` model has no `story`, `useOfFunds`, `risks`, `updates` or `coverImage`).
- **Why it matters:** the artist fills in story, use-of-funds lines, risks and milestones in the wizard (`pages/campaign/Details.tsx:79-81`, `components/campaign/CampaignFanView.tsx:17,27`). The page fans actually see ignores all of it and shows boilerplate instead. A fan comparing two campaigns sees the same budget breakdown on both, which reads as fake. That works directly against the "escrow, transparency, verified artist" positioning. The preview (`CampaignFanView`) and the live public page are two different renderers, so "exactly as fans will see it" (`pages/campaign/Preview.tsx:41`) is not true.
- **Fix:** add story, use of funds, risks, cover, video, updates and FAQ to `Campaign`. Render `/campaigns/:id` with the same `CampaignFanView` component the wizard preview uses. Show real updates with an "Updates (n)" count, and hide sections that are empty rather than filling them in.

### 7. [CONCERN] There's no conversation layer: comment buttons are dead and campaigns have no comments or Q&A
- **Target:** `apps/web/src/pages/fan/HomeFeed.tsx:234-238` (comments `<Button>` with no handler); `apps/web/src/pages/campaigns/CampaignDetail.tsx:153` (tabs are Story, Updates, FAQ only; no Comments, no "Ask the artist").
- **Why it matters:** Kickstarter's comments and Patreon's posts are where backers build trust and artists keep momentum during a 30-day campaign. Trending on the home feed is *ranked by comments* (`HomeFeed.tsx:61`), yet nobody can leave one. Admin has no moderation surface for user-generated content either.
- **Fix:** add backer-badged comments on updates and campaigns, an artist reply and pin, report and hide actions, and a moderation queue in `/admin`.

### 8. [CONCERN] Fans can't manage a pledge after making it: no way to change the perk, change the amount, cancel during the live window, or give a shipping address
- **Target:** `apps/web/src/pages/fan/BackedAndPerks.tsx:150-190` (`BackingRow` is read-only); there's no address book in `pages/settings/Settings.tsx` or `pages/settings/Payments.tsx`; physical perks such as `v3` vinyl (`BackedAndPerks.tsx:51`) never ask where to ship.
- **Why it matters:** "Manage your pledge" is a standard Kickstarter and Indiegogo action, and it's how a fan upgrades a tier late in the campaign (which drives last-48-hour surges). Without a shipping-address flow (a backer survey after funding) physical perks can't actually be fulfilled, and creators have no CSV with addresses. The "Download" and "Track package" controls are also no-ops (`:214` `onClick={() => undefined}`, `:227` `href="#tracking"`).
- **Fix:** add a "Manage pledge" sheet (change perk, add-on, increase, cancel before end) and a post-funding backer survey that collects address, size and variant. Make the creator's backer export include survey answers.

### 9. [CONCERN] Admin is a dashboard of counts, not a workbench: the Disputes and Refunds queues lead nowhere, and there's no assignment, search or bulk action
- **Target:** `apps/web/src/pages/admin/Queues.tsx:43-44` (disputes and refunds have no `to`; `:95` "Summary only — queue view not built yet"); `components/campaign/identityData.ts:95-104` (a chargeback dispute and an 88-backer refund batch are visible but can't be acted on). Fan-filed disputes from `BackedAndPerks.tsx:241` have no staff-side destination.
- **Why it matters:** staff are a primary user in an "enterprise" app. The fan-facing promise ("our team will step in", `CampaignDetail.tsx:36`) rests on a queue that doesn't exist. Ops also needs claim/assign, reviewer workload, an SLA timer per row, saved filters, keyboard triage and a global search (backer email, pledge ID, campaign).
- **Fix:** build `/admin/disputes` and `/admin/refunds` with a case timeline (fan message, artist response, evidence, refund decision, audit) reusing `ReviewWorkspace`'s audit log. Add an assignee column and a "Mine" filter on every queue, and a staff global search.

### 10. [CONCERN] Form controls and card edges fail non-text contrast (WCAG 1.4.11)
- **Target:** `apps/web/src/styles/index.css:22` `--color-line: #2a2c33`; used as the only boundary of inputs, selects and checkboxes in `components/brand/primitives.tsx:174` (`inputBase` `border-line bg-surface-2`) and `:196`, and of every unselected `ChoiceCard`/perk radio (`:213`, `CampaignDetail.tsx:233`).
- **Measured:** line vs canvas **1.41:1**, line vs surface **1.27:1** (needs ≥ 3:1 for UI component boundaries). Text contrast is fine (muted 5.0–6.3:1, gold 7.5–9.4:1).
- **Why it matters:** low-vision users on the dark theme can't find where an input or a perk option starts. This is the kind of thing an enterprise accessibility audit flags immediately.
- **Fix:** add a `--color-line-strong` (around `#5a5e68`, ≥ 3:1 on surface-2) for interactive control borders and keep `line` for decorative dividers only.

### 11. [CONCERN] Loading and error states exist on one page; the other ~90 routes render data instantly or nothing
- **Target:** `?state=loading|error` is implemented only in `apps/web/src/pages/discover/Explore.tsx:32`; `grep Skeleton|state=loading` finds no other page. `CampaignDetail`, `HomeFeed`, `BackedAndPerks`, creator `Dashboard`, `CampaignLive`, `Payouts` and admin `Queues` have no pending, failed-fetch or retry UI. Payouts has no "failed payout / bank rejected" status (`pages/creator/Payouts.tsx:28-32` only scheduled, in transit, paid).
- **Why it matters:** once `apps/api` is wired, every one of these screens will flash empty or crash on slow networks. Money screens especially need a calm "We couldn't load your payouts. Your money is safe. Retry" state, and a failed-payout state with "Update bank details".
- **Fix:** add shared `PageSkeleton`/`ErrorState` primitives (or route `errorElement`s and Suspense boundaries) to the brand kit, and make loading and error states part of the page definition of done in `docs/BUILD_CONVENTIONS.md`.

### 12. [CONCERN] The web app has no tests, so nothing checks the UX, accessibility or flows
- **Target:** `apps/web/package.json` scripts: `dev`, `build`, `preview`, `typecheck`, `lint:copy`, and no `test`. Repo-wide tests are 4 files / ~310 lines in `apps/api/test/*` and `packages/shared/test/rules.test.ts`. Root `pnpm test` (`package.json`) silently skips the web package.
- **Why it matters:** 93 routes and roughly 16.6k lines of page code are protected only by `tsc` and a banned-words linter. The defects above (lost `next`/`perk`, dead buttons, missing reduced-motion, contrast) are exactly what automated a11y and e2e tests catch. "Screenshot looks finished" (BUILD_CONVENTIONS DoD #3) is a visual check, not a quality gate.
- **Fix:** add Vitest + Testing Library for brand primitives and form validation, Playwright for the five golden journeys (discover → back → confirm → perk; sign-up with `next`; create → submit → admin approve; live → payout; dispute → resolve), and `@axe-core/playwright` on every manifest route in CI.

### 13. [BLOCKER] On a campaign's final day the page says "Campaign ended" and disables backing, during the busiest hours of the campaign
- **Target:** `apps/web/src/lib/format.ts:38-40` (`daysUntil` = `ceil((localMidnight(endsOn) - now)/day)`); `apps/web/src/pages/campaigns/CampaignDetail.tsx:67` (`ended = days <= 0`), `:118-119`, `:176` (perks disabled); `components/brand/primitives.tsx:128` (`"Ended"` when `daysLeft <= 0`).
- **Repro:** `endsOn: "2026-10-03"`, now = 2026-10-03 10:00 local → `daysUntil` returns `-0` → the CTA reads "Campaign ended" and is disabled, all perk radios are disabled, and cards show "Ended". Kickstarter campaigns typically see a large share of pledges in the final 48 hours. This turns the final-day rush off.
- **Also:** `endsOn` is a date with no time or timezone, so the deadline means a different instant for every viewer. For an all-or-nothing escrow product the deadline must be a single UTC instant shown in the viewer's local time ("Ends Fri 11:59 PM ET").
- **Fix:** store `endsAt` as an ISO instant. Compute `msLeft` and show "Ends in 6h 12m" or "Last day" under 24h, and only mark a campaign ended when `msLeft <= 0` or the server status says so.

### 14. [CONCERN] Single locale and currency are hard-coded, with no path to international fans
- **Target:** `apps/web/src/lib/format.ts:5-7,12-14,21,35` (`"en-US"` + `"USD"` hard-coded); 30 `toLocaleString()` calls in pages and components bypass the formatter (for example `primitives.tsx:126`, `pages/creator/CampaignLive.tsx:117`); about 34 literal `$N` strings in page copy (for example `pages/campaign/Basics.tsx:97`); all UI strings are inline JSX, with no message catalog.
- **Why it matters:** the artists named in the mock data (Afrobeats, highlife) have fan bases outside the US. Bandcamp and Patreon show approximate local-currency prices. An enterprise-grade platform needs at least a display-currency estimate ("≈ €23"), locale-aware dates and numbers, and extractable strings before a second language is possible. Changing this later means editing ~16k lines.
- **Fix:** route every number through `format.ts` with a `locale` and `currency` from context, show a "Charged in USD" note on checkout, and adopt an i18n library (FormatJS/Lingui) now while strings are still mock-only.

### 15. [NIT] Dark-only theme, and the manifest and meta description reuse the "Own the future" tagline in Layer 1 contexts
- **Target:** `apps/web/index.html:2` (`class="dark"` forced), `styles/index.css:94` (`color-scheme: dark`, no light tokens); `index.html:6` and `public/site.webmanifest:4` `"Fund the culture. Own the future."`; `layouts/shells.tsx:84`.
- **Why it matters:** "Own the future" next to a page that says "Backing … doesn't give you … ownership" (`CampaignDetail.tsx:31-32`) is a mixed message for Layer 1 fans, and it's what link previews and app installs will show. Dark-only is a deliberate brand choice, but it hurts readability outdoors on mobile and for some low-vision users.
- **Fix:** use a Layer-1-safe description such as "Back independent artists and get real perks. All-or-nothing, held in escrow." Consider offering a light theme as an accessibility setting.

## What's genuinely good (so it isn't lost in fixes)
- Copy is calm, specific and human ("Your money waits in escrow. Not with us, and not with the artist." `Landing.tsx:158`). The refund and escrow promise is consistent across Landing, CampaignDetail, BackedAndPerks and the footer.
- The perk picker uses real `role="radio"` semantics, shows scarcity ("12 of 50 left"), handles sold-out, and the CTA states the exact amount (`CampaignDetail.tsx:190`).
- The wizard moves focus to the first invalid field (`components/campaign/WizardFrame.tsx:72-78`), and the preview has creator attestations.
- The admin review workspace has an append-only audit log and requires a note on flagged checks (`components/campaign/ReviewWorkspace.tsx:4,240`).
- Text contrast passes AA comfortably, and focus rings are global and visible (`styles/index.css:141`).

## Highly desirable features

1. **One-tap checkout with Apple Pay / Google Pay and guest backing.** *Serves fans; drives artist conversion.* Most campaign traffic arrives on mobile from social links. Making people create an account → verify email → 6 onboarding steps before paying (finding 1) loses most impulse backers. Wallet pay plus "create account after" is the single biggest conversion lever.
2. **Dynamic OG share cards and a "Share kit" for artists.** *Serves artists and growth.* Auto-generated images (story, square, link card) showing live progress ("72% · 3 days left"), plus pre-written posts, a QR code for merch tables, and an embeddable widget for Linktree/websites. This is the main way crowdfunding campaigns get discovered.
3. **Referral attribution and "Backers who brought backers".** *Serves artists, with a non-monetary reward for fans.* Per-fan share links that show artists which fans and channels drove pledges (a referral dashboard like Kickstarter's), plus engagement badges for fans who recruit others. This fits the decided badges-not-dollars rule (CONSOLIDATION open question 2).
4. **Stretch goals, add-ons and a "last 48 hours" mode.** *Serves artists (higher average pledge) and fans (more value).* Unlockable stretch goals shown on the progress bar, perk add-ons at checkout, and urgency UI with an hour countdown and a final-day push notification to followers who haven't backed.
5. **Post-funding backer survey and a fulfillment hub.** *Serves artists, fans and staff.* Collect addresses, sizes and variants, lock and export them, bulk-mark shipped with tracking import, and send automatic "your perk shipped" notifications. This turns the perk vault from a mock into the fulfillment backbone, and it's the most common place creators fail on other platforms.
6. **Real-time notification center plus email and web push digests.** *Serves everyone.* Funded, refunded, perk shipped, new backer, milestone released, revision requested, payout failed. Patreon-style weekly digests bring fans back between campaigns. (Finding 4.)
7. **Campaign comments, backer-only updates and "Ask the artist".** *Serves fans (connection) and artists (trust, retention).* Backer badges on comments, artist-pinned answers that become FAQ entries, and staff moderation tools. Backer-only updates give fans a reason to come back after pledging.
8. **Follow and "Notify me on launch" for upcoming campaigns.** *Serves artists, who launch with day-one momentum.* A pre-launch page collecting followers lets artists open strong. Kickstarter's pre-launch pages are a proven driver of early funding, and early funding predicts success.
9. **Creator analytics: funnel, traffic sources, conversion by perk.** *Serves artists.* Page views → perk selected → checkout → backed, broken down by referrer and perk. It tells an artist which perk to add or reprice mid-campaign. The current dashboard shows totals only (`pages/creator/Dashboard.tsx:168-214`).
10. **Staff case management and a 360° backer/artist view.** *Serves staff (enterprise ops).* Unified search by email, pledge or card last-4, an account timeline (pledges, refunds, disputes, KYC, comms), assignment and SLAs, macros, and a refund tool that shows its effect on the ledger. Without it, disputes and chargebacks don't scale (finding 9).
11. **Localized display currency and multilingual UI.** *Serves international fans and diaspora-heavy genres.* Approximate local prices and locale-aware dates first, then string translation. (Finding 14.)
12. **Accessibility settings: reduced motion, higher-contrast borders, light theme.** *Serves low-vision and motion-sensitive users, and enterprise and partner procurement (VPAT).* (Findings 5, 10, 15.)

---
**Tally:** 15 findings — 4 BLOCKER (1, 2, 6, 13), 10 CONCERN, 1 NIT — plus 12 highly desirable features.
