# G1 Council Review: PRAGMATIST seat

**Gate:** G1 (requirements) · **Artifacts:** `specs/00-brief.md`, `specs/01-requirements.md` · **Date:** 2026-10-03 · **Tier:** Enterprise
**Lens:** priority honesty, shippability for this team (one founder working evenings, plus agents), scope consistency, and effort.

**Bottom line:** the content is right. The prioritisation isn't usable yet. The P0 set holds 37 FRs and 48 NFR rows, and it's defined as "required before a beta that takes real money". Under the E1 card B default there is no real money until a custodian signs, which is 6–12 months away. So "P0" currently means "everything we need in a year", not "what we build next". The fix is to split P0 into two tiers, not to delete requirements.

## Walking skeleton (recommended first milestone, P0a core, ≈ 6–8 weeks)
**Goal:** one Fund My Show campaign from draft to release, and one from draft to refund, run in the browser on Stripe **test mode**, observable and recoverable. No live money.

**In:**
1. **Auth:** Supabase magic-link sign-in. 18+ attestation and terms acceptance (placeholder docs, versioned) recorded server-side. Guarded routes. (FR-ID-001 partial, FR-ID-006, FR-PRV-001 mechanics, FR-ID-005 guards)
2. **Artist:** create and edit a draft with perks and 2 tranches, then submit (FR-CMP-001, FR-CMP-002). Stripe Connect test onboarding stands in for FR-ID-004.
3. **Review:** one generic staff queue. Wayne approves in **single-operator mode**, with an audited reason and reviewer ≠ owner enforced (because Wayne won't own campaigns). Publish sets a UTC deadline instant. (FR-ADM-001 slice, FR-CMP-002, FR-BCK-004)
4. **Fan:** `/explore`, then a truthful campaign page with the same renderer as the wizard preview, then checkout with Stripe Elements. Perk reservation with expiry, claim-first idempotency, choice survives sign-in, confirmation. (FR-CMP-003, FR-BCK-001/002, FR-PAY-001/006)
5. **Money correctness:** webhook inbox, auto-refund of any capture that can't be applied, two-phase outbound with provider lookup and dead-letter, and `tranche.verified` → release. (FR-PAY-002/003/004/005)
6. **Single-backing refund** from the staff queue (FR-DSP-001, no threshold).
7. **My backings** with the money state derived from the ledger (FR-BCK-005 + the FR-PAY-009 fan slice).
8. **Five emails:** receipt, funded, failed + refund started, refund completed, milestone released. (FR-NTF-001 slice)
9. **Ops floor:** a separate prod Supabase project with PITR, no seeded staff, column-grant fix plus RLS tests, Sentry, worker heartbeat alert, fail-closed env. (NFR-OPS-01/04/06, NFR-SEC-01/04/07)
10. **Proof:** the money-path concurrency tests (NFR-QA-01 core) and two Playwright journeys (back → release, back → refund) in CI. Funnel and `ref` events are logged from day one.

**Out (next milestones):** disputes/chargebacks (test-mode Stripe can simulate them, so they come next), tax and sanctions, MFA beyond staff, staff SSO and separate origin, custodian reconciliation, social image cards, notifications center, community, stretch goals, PDF receipts, load tests, pen test.

**Exit test:** two seeded artists and five real people back two campaigns on test cards. One campaign funds and releases both tranches. One fails and refunds everyone. Ledger ↔ Stripe test balance transactions diff to zero, and every step shows up in Sentry/audit with one correlation id.

## Recommendation
**Pass with conditions** once the two blockers are fixed in the document: (1) split P0 into P0a and P0b, with the walking skeleton above as the first P0a milestone, and (2) add the single-operator-mode requirement with its exit condition. Track the concerns as conditions for G2. Start the counsel, custodian and vendor asks now. Their calendars, not the code, set the real-money date.
