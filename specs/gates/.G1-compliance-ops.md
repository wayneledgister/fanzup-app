# G1 Council — Compliance/Ops Seat · FanZuP v2 requirements

**Seat:** Compliance / Operations (regulatory obligations, consumer protection, privacy, retention, SLOs, recovery, alerting) · **Date:** 2026-10-03 · **Tier:** Enterprise
**Artifacts reviewed:** `specs/00-brief.md`, `specs/01-requirements.md`
**Upstream checked against:** `gates/E1-enterprise-readiness.md`, `gates/.E1-compliance-ops.md` (F1–F19, NFR-COMP-01..18, NFR-OPS-01..10, operational baseline), `packages/shared/src/policy.ts`.

**Summary.** The money-side obligations from E1 survived well. Custody memo, three-way reconciliation, two-phase outbound, dispute lifecycle, suspension, tax-identity gate, sanctions screening and recorded acceptance all have IDs, acceptance criteria and a trace back to E1. Two things stop the gate. First, an entire tax obligation that the spec makes unavoidable is missing: **sales tax on physical and ticket perks**. It changes the P0 checkout total and the flow of funds. Second, several obligations E1 gated "before real money" or "before real users" were quietly moved to P1, including one that was part of E1 blocker B2's resolution. The rest are concerns, mostly around the growth features. Commercial email, trackers and consent, artist access to backer data, and promotion/endorsement rules each create obligations the spec doesn't capture. The ops section also defers its alert thresholds to a council working note instead of stating them.

| 1 | [BLOCKER] | **Sales tax on reward perks is not addressed anywhere.** Physical perks (FR-FUL-001/002) and show tickets (FR-FUL-004) are taxable sales of goods or admissions in most states. Post-*Wayfair* marketplace-facilitator laws can make the *platform* responsible for collecting and remitting tax on sales it facilitates, whether or not it holds the funds. FR-BCK-001 defines the checkout total as "perk … optional extra amount, total in USD", with no tax line, no taxable/non-taxable split between perk value and the pure-support portion, and no nexus or threshold tracking. Under "FanZuP never holds cash" (brief §Constraints), *who* collects and remits is a flow-of-funds question of the same kind as card B and Q7. The current tax vendor scope (FR-TAX-001/002) covers only W-9/1099. A missing tax line can't be added after launch without repricing every live campaign and changing ledger accounts (tax payable). **Resolve:** add a brief open question (counsel: is FanZuP a marketplace facilitator for reward perks, and in which states?). Add `FR-TAX-005` with `[NEEDS CLARIFICATION]`: checkout computes and shows tax where required; the ledger posts tax collected to its own payable account held by the custodian/processor; perk definitions carry a tax category; nexus thresholds are tracked per state. Add tax remittance to the FR-PAY-007 reconciliation scope. | FR-BCK-001, FR-FUL-001/004, FR-TAX-001/002, FR-PAY-007; brief §Constraints, §Open questions |

Restore NFR-COMP-13 (written retention schedule) to P0. | 3 | [CONCERN] | **Commercial email obligations (CAN-SPAM) aren't requirements, and messages aren't classified.** FR-NTF-001 says only "transactional messages can't be switched off; marketing messages can". The growth features send messages whose primary purpose is promotional: launch alerts to followers (FR-CMP-005), final-48-hours "back now" reminders (FR-CMP-006), weekly digests (FR-NTF-003), artist updates emailed to backers (FR-COM-001) and share prompts. Nothing requires: a classification of every template as transactional/relationship vs commercial; a working one-click unsubscribe honoured within 10 business days; a physical postal address and accurate sender/subject in commercial mail; a global suppression list that survives account deletion (as a hash); a recorded opt-in (time, source, wording) for "notify me", digests and push; or a rule on whether a signed-out visitor can follow a pre-launch page by email (double opt-in, 18+). Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules (one-click `List-Unsubscribe`, complaint rate < 0.3%) also apply to transactional deliverability. **Condition:** add `FR-NTF-004 Message classification & marketing consent` with these ACs; FR-CMP-005/006 and FR-NTF-003 cite it. | FR-NTF-001/003, FR-CMP-005/006, FR-COM-001, FR-GRO-004 |
| 4 | [CONCERN] | **Trackers, cookies and opt-out signals are one vague line.** FR-ANL-002 says analytics "respects consent where state law requires it". FR-ANL-001 (views by source), FR-GRO-003 (last-touch attribution over a policy window, which implies a persistent identifier), FR-GRO-001 (social cards) and FR-GRO-002 (embeddable widget on third-party sites) all imply client-side tracking. Missing: a tracker/vendor inventory; a rule that no advertising pixels or third-party scripts run on checkout or identity pages (this also serves PCI DSS 4.0 6.4.3 script inventory); whether any analytics or attribution counts as "sale/sharing" or targeted advertising under CCPA/CPRA and the other state laws; Global Privacy Control honoured at the browser (NFR-COMP-14 mentions preference signals but has no FR); a cookie notice/consent mechanism where required; and the embed setting no cookies on host pages. **Condition:** add `FR-PRV-005 Tracking & consent` (inventory, GPC, no third-party scripts on payment/identity pages, attribution identifier first-party with stated lifetime) at P0 for whatever analytics ships in beta. | FR-ANL-001/002, FR-GRO-001/002/003, NFR-COMP-14 |
| 5 | [CONCERN] | **Artists receive backer personal data, and the obligations of that sharing aren't captured.** Artists get shipping addresses and survey answers (FR-FUL-001), a backer list, per-source attribution (FR-GRO-003), fan-raised problem threads (FR-DSP-003) and comment identities, and can export files. That makes the artist an independent recipient of consumer data. Nothing requires: the Privacy Notice to disclose sharing with artists by category; the Artist Campaign Agreement to restrict artists to fulfilment use only (no adding backers to the artist's own mailing list without separate opt-in, deletion after fulfilment, a breach duty); checkout to tell the fan at the point of collection that the address goes to the artist; or survey fields to be limited, so artists can't collect DOB, phone and similar. The opt-in rules for follows (FR-GRO-004) and the backer wall (FR-GRO-005) also need recorded consent, not only a toggle. **Condition:** add ACs to FR-PRV-001 (disclosure + artist data-use clause in the Artist Agreement), FR-BCK-001 (notice at collection) and FR-FUL-001 (typed survey fields, artist-side deletion after lock + N days). | FR-FUL-001, FR-GRO-003/004/005, FR-DSP-003, FR-PRV-001, FR-BCK-001 |
| 6 | [CONCERN] | **Retention and deletion conflict with the immutability requirements, and the schedule comes after the data.** NFR-COMP-12 exports audit and ledger rows to object-locked storage for ≥ 7 years. NFR-COMP-11 puts IP, user agent and before/after values (which can include names, emails and addresses) into those rows. NFR-OPS-03 copies backings and campaigns to a second provider. FR-PRV-003 promises PII is "removed or pseudonymised", but nothing says what PII may enter immutable or off-platform copies, how long logical backups live, or that deletion is re-applied after a restore (NFR-OPS-02 drill). Also unpurged: idempotency rows (Comp F19 asked for a 30–90 day TTL), the outbox, raw provider event payloads (FR-PAY-002), message logs (FR-NTF-001), comment and report history. NFR-COMP-13 (the schedule) is P1, while P0 features already collect all of it. **Condition:** retention schedule P0 (see finding 2) with a row per data class, including backups and exports. Audit/export rows carry pseudonymous ids and hashed IP, not raw PII in before/after. Add a "deletion replay after restore" AC to NFR-OPS-02. | NFR-COMP-11/12/13, NFR-OPS-02/03, FR-PRV-003, FR-PAY-002, FR-NTF-001 |

| 9 | [CONCERN] | **Sanctions hits and undeliverable refunds have no compliant end state.** FR-TAX-003 says a potential match "blocks money movement … and opens a review case". FR-PAY-007 says refunds to fans continue during a recon pause, and FR-PAY-003 auto-refunds unapplied captures. A refund to a confirmed SDN backer is itself a prohibited transfer. Funds may need to be *blocked* (not returned) and reported to OFAC within 10 business days, with annual blocked-property reporting, and the custodian may own that step. None of this is a requirement, and the refund paths have no sanctions carve-out. Separately, refunds that can never land (closed card, dead-lettered after 8 attempts per FR-PAY-004) leave money owed to a consumer with no path. The spec needs an alternate refund method and eventual state unclaimed-property (escheat) handling, with the custodian/FanZuP split stated. FR-TAX-003 also says "when lists update" while NFR-COMP-09 says "daily"; align them. **Condition:** add ACs to FR-TAX-003 (block vs reject, OFAC reporting owner, refund carve-out) and a new FR-DSP item for undeliverable refunds and escheatment. | FR-TAX-003, FR-PAY-003/004/007, NFR-COMP-09 |
