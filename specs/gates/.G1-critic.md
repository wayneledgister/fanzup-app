# G1 — Critic seat · FanZuP v2 requirements · 2026-10-03
**Artifacts:** `specs/00-brief.md`, `specs/01-requirements.md` · **Upstream:** `gates/E1-enterprise-readiness.md`, repo at `a165610` · **Tier:** Enterprise
**Rubric:** Can a third party test every AC without asking questions? Is solution language smuggled into requirements? Are the `[NEEDS CLARIFICATION]` items genuine and complete? Are the user stories good for fans, artists and staff? Is the P0/P1/P2 split defensible?

**Seat verdict: REVISE (1 blocker).** Most ACs are Given/When/Then with numbers. For a draft written while the founder was away, that is unusually testable, and the fan stories are written by someone who has watched a fan abandon a checkout. Two things stop it passing. First, the priority scheme is defined against an event (a beta taking real money) that the default custody posture rules out. Second, a run of P0 money controls hang on "policy" values nobody has set. The three `[NEEDS CLARIFICATION]` tags are genuine, but they aren't the whole list.

## Findings
| Sev | Finding | Ref |
|---|---|---|
| [BLOCKER] | **The P0 definition can't be applied under the default custody posture.** P0 means "required before a beta that takes real money from real fans." The default for E1 card B (the brief proceeds on it) is *no live money until a custodian signs, 6–12 months out*, with the beta run in test mode. So the beta that real users will actually touch has no defined requirement set. Should a test-mode beta with real sign-ups need FR-TAX-001 W-9 gating? NFR-SEC-10's pen test? FR-PRV-001 counsel-approved terms (it collects real PII and 18+ attestations)? A planner can't sequence the work and a tester can't say what "beta-ready" means. Building against it means either doing 6–12 months of P0 before any user sees the product or silently skipping controls. **Resolve before pass:** split P0 into P0a (real users, test-mode money: identity, privacy, security headers, a11y, the full flow wired) and P0b (before live money: custody, recon, tax, sanctions, pen test, disputes), and re-tag each requirement. | Requirements §Priorities; brief Q2; E1 card B; NFR-SEC-10; FR-TAX-001/003; FR-PRV-001 |
| [CONCERN] | **P0 money controls can't be tested because their thresholds don't exist.** A tester can't write pass/fail for any of these: the "fixed hold window (policy default)" in FR-PAY-001; "a policy threshold" for a second refund approval in FR-DSP-001; "a policy threshold" dispute ratio in FR-DSP-004; "policy thresholds" for two approvers in FR-ID-003; "policy limits" and the "policy-set number" in FR-TAX-004; the "policy window" in FR-GRO-003; and the "policy SLA" in FR-DSP-003. None of these keys exists in `packages/shared/src/policy.ts`. **Condition:** each AC names its policy key, and the key has a proposed value, before G2. | FR-PAY-001; FR-DSP-001/003/004; FR-ID-003; FR-TAX-004; FR-GRO-003 |
| [CONCERN] | **Unmarked ambiguities that fork the design.** (1) **When is the fan charged?** At pledge, held for up to 60 days, or at the deadline as on Kickstarter? The brief's "every dollar waits with a custodian" implies charge-at-pledge. That drives non-refundable card fees on the roughly 60% of campaigns expected to fail (brief success-rate target 40%), FR-PAY-001's hold design, and FR-BCK-003. It should be a recorded decision, not an inference. (2) **Who is the second approver** in every dual-control AC, given one founder? (3) FR-TAX-003 screens backers "by the provider *or* FanZuP at least on name and country", which isn't a testable requirement until someone picks one. (4) FR-FUL-001 and NFR-SEC-08 are "P0 if any beta campaign has physical perks": that is a decision dressed as a condition. (5) **Guest checkout**, yes or no (next finding). **Condition:** add these to the `[NEEDS CLARIFICATION]` list with an owner. | Brief one-liner, Q2; FR-PAY-001; FR-BCK-003; FR-CMP-007; FR-ID-003; FR-TAX-003; FR-FUL-001; NFR-SEC-08 |
| [CONCERN] | **The P0 fan journey works against its own conversion target.** The fan persona "arrives from an artist's social link; never heard of FanZuP", and the target is ≥ 45% mobile checkout conversion. FR-BCK-002 still requires full account creation (verified email per FR-ID-001, password/passkey, attestation) *before* paying. Email verification before the first backing means leaving the app for an inbox mid-checkout. The story is good ("my choice survives sign-up"), but it optimises the detour instead of asking whether the detour is needed. **Condition:** either record guest/deferred-account checkout (account claimed from the receipt email) as a considered and rejected option with its reason, or allow verification after the backing, for example before the perk survey. | FR-BCK-002; FR-ID-001 AC1; brief §Personas, §Success metrics |
| [CONCERN] | **The P0 campaign page renders content that nobody can author until P1.** FR-CMP-003 (P0, closing E1 B12) renders the artist's "updates" and milestone progress, but posting an update (FR-COM-001) is P1. The artist persona's stated need "telling fans what's happening" therefore has no P0 path, and B12 was raised precisely because updates were fabricated. FR-FUL-003 (P1) also needs the artist to "give a new date" and notify backers, with no channel to do it. **Condition:** raise a minimal FR-COM-001 (text-only, all-backers updates) to P0, or remove "updates" from FR-CMP-003's P0 AC. | FR-CMP-003; FR-COM-001; FR-FUL-003; brief §Personas (artist) |
| [CONCERN] | **Contradictory numbers leave the tester unsure what to assert.** Capture refunds: 1 hour (FR-PAY-003) vs. 1 business day (NFR-COMP-05). DSAR: "within the legal deadline" (FR-PRV-002) vs. a 45-day SLA (NFR-COMP-14); say which. Pen test: tagged "P1 … before real money if Beta takes live money", which by the P0 definition *is* P0. Tranches: "optional milestone tranches" (FR-CMP-001) vs. `POLICY.campaign.minTranches = 2`. **Condition:** one number per obligation, owned by the FR; NFR summaries defer to it. | FR-PAY-003; NFR-COMP-05; FR-PRV-002; NFR-COMP-14; NFR-SEC-10; FR-CMP-001 |
| [CONCERN] | **Solution language in requirements.** "unique on provider event id" (FR-PAY-002), "an 'initiated' record exists before the provider call" (FR-PAY-004), "enforced by the API and the database" (FR-PLT-001), "All writes go through the API" (NFR-SEC-02), "database-backed" queues (NFR-OPS-08), "against real Postgres" (NFR-QA-01), "served separately … staff single sign-on" (FR-ID-005), and "the 46 unused generic UI components" (NFR-QA-05, which is a cleanup task, not a requirement). Some are deliberate E1 constraints, which is fine, but then they should be labelled **Constraint (E1 Cx)** so that G2 doesn't treat them as negotiable outcomes, and the outcome (exactly-once processing, no double execution) should be the testable AC. **Condition:** restate each as outcome + labelled constraint, and move NFR-QA-05 to the plan. | FR-PAY-002/004; FR-PLT-001; FR-ID-005; NFR-SEC-02; NFR-OPS-08; NFR-QA-01/05 |
| [CONCERN] | **ACs that need a judge, not a tester.** These depend on someone's opinion: "a specific, human error" (FR-BCK-001), "human messages" (FR-ID-001), "keyboard triage on desktop" (FR-ADM-001), "shows nothing generic or invented" (FR-CMP-003), "honest status message" (FR-CMP-007), "a material change" (FR-PRV-001), and "respects consent where state law requires it" (FR-ANL-002). **Condition:** replace each with an observable check, e.g. "each processor decline code maps to a distinct copy string in the catalogue; no screen shows a raw provider message"; "every rendered section's text comes from the campaign record"; "a counsel-maintained list of material-change triggers". | FR-BCK-001; FR-ID-001; FR-ADM-001; FR-CMP-003; FR-CMP-007; FR-PRV-001; FR-ANL-002 |
| [CONCERN] | **The fan's self-serve remedies are P1 while the staff remedies are P0.** Fans can't cancel during the live window (FR-BCK-003, P1) or raise a perk problem in-app (FR-DSP-003, P1) during a real-money beta. The only path is emailing support or calling the bank. That is exactly the chargeback driver the brief's < 0.5% dispute target is meant to suppress. **Condition:** make FR-DSP-003's minimal form (report → case → support alert) P0, or state that beta fans get a documented support-email path with the same SLA. | FR-BCK-003; FR-DSP-003; brief §Success metrics (dispute ratio) |
| [NIT] | **Stories written from the operator's point of view.** FR-PAY-008, FR-ID-003, FR-ID-005, FR-PLT-001 and FR-DSP-005 are "As Wayne, I want…". These are controls, not user value. Write them as constraints, or give them the beneficiary (e.g. "As a fan, I want my money only ever handled under an approved custody model…"). | FR-PAY-008; FR-ID-003; FR-ID-005; FR-PLT-001; FR-DSP-005 |
| [NIT] | **Approved-but-not-launched campaigns have no expiry.** FR-CMP-002 lets the artist "choose when to go live" with no limit. A campaign approved in March and launched in September carries stale perk dates and stale sanctions and identity checks. Add a re-review window. | FR-CMP-002; FR-TAX-003 |
| [NIT] | **Success metrics lack a denominator or source definition.** "Perks delivered by their promised date ≥ 90%" doesn't say whether that's per perk unit or per backing, or whether the original or the revised date (FR-FUL-003) counts. "Share of backings via a shared link" depends on FR-GRO-003, which is P1, but the metric is "measured from day 1". | Brief §Success metrics; FR-GRO-003; FR-ANL-002 |
