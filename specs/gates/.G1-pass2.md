## Recommended pass-2 verdict: **REVISE** (narrow)
The revision is substantial and mostly faithful. Five of seven blockers and half the conditions are fully closed, and no new blocker was introduced. Two things still stop an approval.

1. **Blocker 7 (PRD trace) is still open, and only Wayne can close it.** The brief itself makes it a G1 sign-off precondition, and the revision added more untraced product decisions. The council can't mark it resolved.
2. **Blocker 2 is partly resolved.** Runtime controls now work for one operator (FR-ID-007), but the P0a change-management controls (NFR-OPS-07, NFR-SEC-09) still need two humans (N3), and some money actions sit outside single-operator mode (N4).

**Document edits to close pass 3 (small, ≈ half a day):** N2 (FR-ID-001 vs FR-BCK-002), N3 (single-maintainer rule), N4 (complete the privileged-action list, recon override, break-glass refund limit), plus the open halves of conditions 2 (DSAR deadline, pen-test timing, tranche count) and 10 (P0a funnel-event AC). The other concerns (N1, N5–N10, N14) and conditions 1, 4, 6 and 12 can carry to G2 as conditions.

**Path to approval without waiting for the PRDs (founder's call, not the council's):** if Wayne explicitly waives Q1 **for M1 only**, the council would accept **APPROVE WITH CONDITIONS scoped to M1**. M1 is test-mode money-path mechanics that the PRDs are unlikely to contradict. The PRD trace would then gate M2 (real users), and the carried conditions above would apply. Without that waiver, the honest verdict stays REVISE.

## Skeptic's strongest reason not to proceed (updated)
The revision fixed the document's internal honesty: it now admits there's one operator and no money for months, and it scopes controls to match. It still hasn't touched the two outside facts everything rests on. The PRDs that outrank this spec are still unread, and the revision made *more* product decisions on top of them: light-account checkout, discovery order, attribution window, charge timing. And no fan or artist has seen any of it. Splitting P0 didn't shrink the first beta. It moved most of the old P0 to a test-money milestone whose headline metric (≥ 45% conversion) can't be measured on test cards, so the funnel will be frozen for M3 before the first real conversion number exists. Cheapest honest move: Wayne commits the PRD folder (an afternoon), the council traces it, M1 starts as the walking skeleton, and M2 is cut down to what a five-artist, test-card beta actually needs, with a test-mode participation promise written down.
